## Read this first: three assignment premises turned out to be wrong

The work order asked me to re-open the science and pivot if the evidence
contradicted the plan. It does, in three places.

**1. DS010 is not "public but possibly imputed". It is embargoed.**
The assignment framed the DS010 question as raw-vs-imputed contact bytes. The
question does not arise. Both SubSeries carrying the data are private, verbatim
from GEO:

> "Accession GSE308668 is currently private and is scheduled to be released on
> **Feb 01, 2027**." — identical for GSE308906.

Both FTP supplementary directories return `Object not found!`. The only public
artifact under SuperSeries `GSE308132` is a 12,793-byte metadata table.
`DS010_CONSTRUCTION_USE = BLOCKED_BY_EMBARGO__NOT_BY_IMPUTATION`.

**2. The DS010 companion multiome is the same embargo.** It is one of those two
private SubSeries — *which one is not publicly determinable*. So the prospective
zero-overlap donor split cannot be frozen. I declined to freeze one anyway: it
would buy nothing, because the split can be frozen the moment the data is public
and still before any outcome is inspected. Recorded **DEFERRED**, not done.

**3. Yang's public pcHi-C interactions could not be located.** `GSE173316` is
public with 26 samples, but its series-level RAW tar (24,944,640 bytes, sha256
`5f3474e5…`) contains **only 8 RSEM gene-expression results and 2 CRISPR count
matrices**. No pcHi-C interaction file at series level. Recorded
`ARTIFACT_UNLOCATED` rather than assumed.

The consequence is the assignment's own fallback: **a Nott-centered object**, not
a Nott ∩ DS010 intersection.

### Task 8 — the identifiability benchmark, and the boundary that matters
All five negatives rejected at donors=18, seeds=24; margin +0.01592 with
**NEG2 hidden quality as the binding ceiling**. The twin is
`NON-IDENTIFIABLE`: its score equals POS1's exactly, which is the correct
invariant — a difference would be a *leak*.

The result that actually constrains the claim is the sweep over the
**unobservable** share of hidden quality:

| orthogonal share b | NEG2 q90 | margin |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | +0.00201 | +0.02254 |
| 0.4 | +0.01404 | +0.02254 |
| 0.8 | +0.02771 | +0.01557 |
| **1.6** | **+0.04137** | **+0.00191** |
| 3.2 | +0.03695 | +0.00634 |

At b=0 the latent is fully observable and residualization removes it completely
— a positive control on the mechanism. By **b=1.6 the margin collapses to
+0.002**, essentially zero. So the honest claim is *"rejects hidden quality up to
a bounded unobservable share"*, **not** *"rejects hidden quality"*. The boundary
is the finding.

Common support cost **59% of linked pairs** (395/960 retained, 334/2,479 strata
usable). Real data should be expected to trim at least this hard.

## Standing cautions carried forward

- **Do not revive the Corces contact route.** V61 measured microglia as the
  worst-matched of 24 clusters, weakest residual after size control, beaten
  2.5× by a *doublet* artifact.
- **A fail is not proof of technical origin.** Frozen in the contract as C10
  before any test: a fail can equally reflect age/state rewiring, contact
  resolution, microglial depth, measurement-support attrition or power.
- **Nott is hg19; accessibility and NIH-CARD are hg38.** Harmonization is a
  gate step with its own round-trip qualification and counted attrition, not a
  preprocessing detail. It is the step most likely to introduce a silent bias.
- **Nott is surgical/young tissue; NIH-CARD is aged postmortem.** That is a
  state-rewiring hazard for interpretation, not a donor-overlap hazard.
- **NIH-CARD is an aging, neurologically normal cohort.** A pass there says
  nothing about disease context.
