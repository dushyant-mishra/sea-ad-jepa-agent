# JEPA claim ladder — V3 prefreeze

Date: 2026-10-06
Status: `PREFREEZE_ONLY__NO_EXECUTION_AUTHORITY`

## Purpose

Prevent evidence at one level from being silently promoted into a stronger biological claim.

## Claim levels

1. `RNA_REPRESENTATION`
   - A lawful representation captures recoverable RNA structure beyond frozen shortcuts.
   - This is the maximum claim available from Stage A alone.

2. `TRANSFERABLE_BIOLOGICAL_STATE`
   - Requires new evidence that the representation transports across prospectively defined biological units and is not explained by measurement/source shortcuts.
   - RNA reconstruction alone cannot establish this level.

3. `REGULATORY_SUPPORT`
   - Requires independent regulatory/chromatin evidence appropriate to the frozen target/representation.
   - RNA/ATAC association by itself is support, not causal regulation.

4. `CAUSAL_PERTURBATIONAL_PREDICTION`
   - Requires intervention evidence under prospectively defined perturbational reliability and exposure rules.
   - Observational association cannot close this transition.

## Promotion firewall

Every adjacent transition requires new, prospectively declared evidence.

Forbidden automatic promotions:

- masked-RNA success -> biological state;
- donor transport -> regulatory mechanism;
- RNA/ATAC concordance -> causal regulation;
- observational prediction -> perturbational/counterfactual validity;
- synthetic planted-truth recovery -> real biological validity.

## External evidence and exposure

External does not mean independent. Prior development exposure, donor/study overlap, pairing class and protected status must be declared before an asset contributes to a claim transition.

An asset used to select a target cannot later be called pristine independent confirmation without explicit exposure accounting.

## Failure / uncertainty language

Use `NOT_TESTED`, `NOT_IDENTIFIABLE`, or `INDETERMINATE` when the required evidence for a transition is absent or confounded. Do not convert missing evidence into PASS.

## Current ceiling

`stage_a_maximum_claim = RNA_REPRESENTATION`

No target or representation is currently qualified even at that level by this document; it freezes only the claim semantics.

## Protected boundaries

`TRAINING=OFF`; Stage A execution not authorized; TEST sealed; Morabito protected; no target/representation/estimand selected.
