## 1. Scientific question

Which target protocol, if any, captures donor-recurrent, source-replicated cell biology that cannot be reduced to address/support/operator structure, is constructed by a genuinely richer teacher, remains predictable from strictly poorer student evidence, behaves sensibly under increasing biological evidence and measurement depth, abstains when unsupported, and transfers beyond the data used to define it?

The tournament selects a target protocol. It does not select a neural architecture, latent width or training run.

## 2. Preconditions for future real execution

A future real tournament is blocked unless all of the following have separately completed and been audited:

1. G6/G7 integrity under exact owner authority;
2. separate corrected TD56/TD57B/TD57C/TD59 replay authority and execution;
3. replay interpretation under the prospectively frozen corrected-replay interpretation rules;
4. at least one relational route remains viable;
5. exact real tournament population, donor identities, source families, operators, support registry, evidence rights, split identities and observation descriptors are frozen;
6. the exact V2 decision family, gate cases, multiplicity family, null seeds and external prefreeze SHA are frozen before candidate outcomes;
7. a qualified V2 executable scaffold proves the machine contract below without opening biology.

## 3. Canonical candidate families

### C1 — `C1_SHARED_EMERGENT`

Simplest learned shared biological state. A rich teacher constructs `Z_shared(c)` from lawful same-cell RNA evidence. No coordinate receives biological authority by name.

Purpose: test whether a stable shared biological state exists **without requiring explicit relational regularization to be the target definition**.

### C2 — `C2_RELATIONAL_SHARED`

C1 plus prospectively frozen broad/mesoscale relational constraints supported by corrected replay.

Purpose: test whether explicit relational continuity adds donor-primary biological information and robustness beyond C1.

C2 may not use nearest-third mining or inherit historical nearest-half as a production constant.

### C3 — `C3_QUERY_MEASURED`

Measured q-conditioned non-q same-cell contextual target. The primary target normalization excludes q scalar. The student receives q address only, never q scalar or deterministic q leakage.

Purpose: provide a directly measured contextual anchor rather than a self-referential learned latent.

### C4 — `C4_STRUCTURED_COMPOSITE`

Current incumbent hypothesis, not winner. Combines:

- C2 shared + relational state;
- C3 measured q-conditioned anchors;
- explicit support/abstention;
- biological-evidence response;
- measurement-depth response.

**No constituent may compensate for another constituent failing or remaining untested on an applicable mandatory gate.** C4 therefore inherits the union of all C2 and C3 applicable gates and adds an increment-over-constituents gate.

### C5 — `C5_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST`

Optional regulatory/chromatin specialist only. It never becomes the universal foundation target. Unsupported cells/queries abstain.

## 4. Baseline/falsifier arms

Every applicable candidate must face prospectively frozen versions of:

- B0 constant/training-mean;
- B1 lawful technical/operator-only;
- B2 address/support/operator shortcut;
- B3 matched wrong-cell value/support null;
- B4 fine-neighborhood wrong-cell null for relational candidates;
- q-count-only for q-conditioned candidates;
- q-address-only for q-conditioned candidates;
- query-agnostic readout;
- wrong-q readout;
- technical+q baseline;
- q-excluded teacher anchor;
- identity+support-only baseline for learned shared targets.

No average score can override a failed mandatory falsifier.

## 5. Promotion-gate catalog

The machine contract must expose the twelve prefreeze promotion concepts explicitly.

### `G1_INTEGRITY_LINEAGE`

Exact bytes, code, population, split, support, operator, teacher/student evidence rights, null seed and prefreeze identities are bound. No post-outcome redesign or relabeling.

### `G2_NONTRIVIAL_BIOLOGICAL_CONTENT`

Beats B0/B1/B2 and applicable B3 controls on donor-primary estimands.

### `G3_CORRECT_CELL_SPECIFICITY`

Beats matched wrong-cell controls; relational candidates also defeat B4 where estimable.

### `G4_DONOR_RECURRENCE`

Donor is the biological replication unit. Repeated cells/queries/coordinates within a donor do not inflate N.

### `G5_SOURCE_REPLICATION`

HVS, NPH52 and SEA_AD are adjudicated explicitly. Source failure cannot be hidden by SEA_AD abundance. Unsupported specialist source combinations may be `NOT_ESTIMABLE`, never silently omitted.

### `G6_TEACHER_INFORMATION_ADVANTAGE`

Teacher target is constructed from richer lawful evidence; student cannot access withheld target information directly or transitively.

### `G7_EVIDENCE_CONVERGENCE`

Where applicable, the target/state shows prospectively defined stabilization as lawful biological evidence increases.

### `G8_MEASUREMENT_ROBUSTNESS`

Measurement-depth or remeasurement changes do not masquerade as biological-state changes. Biological and measurement uncertainty remain distinct.

### `G9_LEARNED_TEACHER_CONTINUITY`

For learned shared targets, rich-state geometry recurs across frozen disjoint views and donor-balanced refits before student success is considered.

### `G10_COLLAPSE_SHORTCUT_RESISTANCE`

Noncollapse is necessary but not sufficient. Candidate must also defeat its prospectively assigned shortcut baselines at the selected checkpoint/target definition.

### `G11_TRANSFER`

Meets every prospectively declared valid level among held-out donor, operator/matrix, study family and technology. Invalid/unavailable levels are `NOT_ESTIMABLE`, not omitted.

### `G12_COVERAGE_ABSTENTION`

Meets the prospectively frozen minimum eligible coverage without forced imputation and reports donor/source/operator/query support plus abstention reasons.

## 7. Exact candidate gate inheritance

The V2 executable contract must make inheritance machine-checkable.

### C1_SHARED_EMERGENT

Requires all twelve promotion gates plus S1–S3.

### C2_RELATIONAL_SHARED

Requires the complete C1 gate set plus R1–R4.

### C3_QUERY_MEASURED

Requires promotion gates G1–G8 and G10–G12; G9 is not required because the primary target is measured rather than a learned shared teacher state. Also requires Q1–Q8.

### C4_STRUCTURED_COMPOSITE

Requires the exact union of the C2 and C3 gate sets plus `C4_INCREMENT_OVER_CONSTITUENTS`.

This inheritance is structural. A V2 validator must reject a contract in which C4 omits any inherited gate even if all supplied results are favorable.

### C5_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST

Specialist-only contract requires G1–G5, G10–G12 and M1–M4. G6–G9 apply only if the specialist instantiation actually creates a teacher/student learned state; such activation requires a separately frozen extension before outcomes.

## 8. Evidence-status semantics

Per gate and per predeclared case, status is exactly one of:

- `PASS`;
- `FAIL`;
- `NOT_ESTIMABLE`.

`NOT_ESTIMABLE` is never serialized as `FAIL` and never projects to `PASS`.

A tournament-level redesign/failure conclusion is only permitted after all foundation candidates have complete adjudication. Missing candidate receipts produce an incomplete-tournament terminal, not a winner or redesign conclusion.

## 9. Donor-primary precision law

Any donor-recurrence sign/permutation helper must bind exact unique donor identity. Bare arrays of repeated cell/query deltas may not be interpreted as donor N.

Repeated target coordinates, cells, evidence fractions or queries from one donor remain dependent observations unless a separately prospectively justified hierarchical model is used.

## 10. Frozen-evidence chain

A future gate result must descend from the exact prospective freeze:

`external prefreeze SHA -> exact gate-case coverage -> gate evidence artifact -> candidate evidence manifest -> candidate receipt -> tournament adjudication`

A self-declared hash inside an object is not sufficient freeze authority. Every downstream coverage/adjudication boundary must receive or inherit the externally frozen parent SHA.

The coverage layer does not invent gate-pass policy. Gate-specific qualified executors must define the estimand and decision rule prospectively and emit artifacts whose schemas are bound to the applicable gate.

## 11. C4 increment rule

C4 may become eligible only if:

1. every inherited C2/C3 applicable gate is PASS;
2. constituent target protocols remain individually interpretable;
3. the prospectively frozen C4 increment estimand shows added donor-primary biological information or robustness beyond the strongest qualified constituent;
4. the increment does not reduce transfer, increase shortcut dependence, collapse coverage, or mix biological with measurement uncertainty.

If C2 or C3 is `FAIL`, C4 cannot rescue it. If a C2/C3 gate required by C4 is `NOT_ESTIMABLE`, C4 is also unresolved on that inherited gate unless a prospectively frozen applicability rule explicitly excludes it.

## 12. Specialist rule

C5 may be accepted as a specialist even if no foundation candidate wins, but it may not change the foundation-target terminal. Regulatory evidence reused during target construction cannot later be called independent validation.

## 13. Tournament terminals

At minimum, the V2 adjudicator must distinguish:

- `TOURNAMENT_INCOMPLETE__FOUNDATION_CANDIDATES_MISSING__NO_TARGET_AUTHORITY`;
- `NO_TARGET_WINNER__FOUNDATION_EVIDENCE_NOT_ESTIMABLE__NO_TARGET_AUTHORITY`;
- `NO_TARGET_WINNER__REDESIGN_BEFORE_PROTECTED_CONFIRMATION`;
- `CANDIDATES_ELIGIBLE_FOR_TARGET_SELECTION__NO_TARGET_AUTHORITY`.

None of these selects a winner automatically.

## 14. Representation and training separation

A target-protocol result never selects architecture, width or checkpoint. Representation tournament and real training each require separate prospective contracts and separate owner authority.
