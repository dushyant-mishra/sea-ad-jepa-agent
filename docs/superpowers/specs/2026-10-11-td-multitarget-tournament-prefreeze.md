# Target-discovery multi-target tournament — prospective design prefreeze

Date: 2026-10-11
Status: `DESIGN_PREFROZEN__NOT_AUTHORIZED_FOR_EXECUTION`

This document prospectively defines the target families, information boundaries, falsifiers and promotion logic to be used **after** corrected G6/G7 integrity and corrected TD56/57B/57C/59 replay are separately authorized, executed and audited. It is deliberately written before corrected biological outcomes are opened.

It does not authorize target-tournament execution or any neural training.

## 1. Tournament question

Which target construction, if any, captures reproducible cell biology that:

- is not reducible to address/support/operator/source structure;
- is constructible by a genuinely richer teacher;
- is predictable by a strictly poorer student;
- recurs across donors and source families;
- behaves sensibly as biological evidence increases;
- remains distinguishable from measurement uncertainty;
- can abstain when unsupported rather than invent values;
- provides broad enough corpus coverage to be a plausible foundation objective?

The tournament selects a **target protocol**, not a pretty latent space.

## 2. Preconditions

Execution is blocked unless all are true:

1. comprehensive G6/G7 successor has independently qualified;
2. explicit owner authority for G6/G7 was issued and both integrity gates passed;
3. separate explicit corrected-biological-replay authority was issued;
4. corrected TD56/57B/57C/59 replay completed and was audited;
5. its outcomes were interpreted under `2026-10-11-td-corrected-replay-interpretation-prefreeze.md` without redesign;
6. the replay outcome leaves at least one relational candidate scientifically viable;
7. exact target-tournament input population, donor splits, support registry and observation-operator descriptors are frozen before candidate outcomes.

If the corrected replay removes relational viability, relational arms below are disabled by the predeclared routing rules rather than rescued.

## 3. Common information law

### Teacher rights

The teacher may consume the richest **lawful, authenticated, same-cell** evidence assigned to a candidate. A teacher receiving query scalar `x_q` must have an otherwise identical q-excluded ablation.

Teacher evidence may include:
- lawful RNA values and physical support state;
- query address;
- explicitly permitted observation-operator descriptors;
- optional separately qualified specialist evidence for specialist-only arms.

Teacher evidence may not include:
- pathology or protected outcome labels;
- arbitrary donor ID embedding as biology;
- unrestricted dataset/matrix ID embeddings;
- post-outcome neighborhood or gene selection;
- target-test donor outcomes during target construction.

### Student rights

The student always receives **strictly less biological evidence** than the teacher target requires.

For q-conditioned arms:
- q address is allowed as conditioning;
- q scalar is forbidden;
- q-derived full-library normalization is forbidden;
- QC features that deterministically leak q are forbidden;
- copied teacher embeddings are forbidden;
- teacher-only modality values are forbidden.

### Measurement/operator channel

Physical acquisition descriptors belong in a separate observation channel `O_t`; they may inform inference/calibration but are not counted as biological target content.

## 4. Common primary units

- Donor is the primary biological replication unit.
- Source family is an explicit replication axis, not a cell-count weight.
- Repeated cells, queries and target coordinates within a donor are dependent observations.
- All summary confidence/uncertainty reporting must respect donor clustering.
- HVS, NPH52 and SEA_AD are reported separately before any combined summary.

## 5. Baseline / falsifier arms

These are mandatory competitors, not optional diagnostics.

### B0 — training-mean / constant target

Measures whether a candidate only improves on a trivial population mean.

### B1 — lawful technical/operator-only model

Uses only frozen physical acquisition descriptors and support summaries permitted in `O_t`.

Purpose: reject targets whose apparent predictability is primarily measurement process.

### B2 — address/support/operator shortcut model

Uses target/query address identity, structural measurability/support and lawful operator descriptors, but no cell-specific biological values.

Purpose: directly challenge the historical H-token shortcut failure.

### B3 — matched wrong-cell value/support null

Preserves as much as possible:
- query/address identity;
- physical support;
- operator/source composition;
- donor-primary weighting;
- marginal scale;
- measurable target coordinates;

while breaking cell-specific biological correspondence using a donor-distinct or cell-distinct lawful reassignment within frozen strata.

### B4 — fine-neighborhood wrong-cell null

For relational candidates, select a wrong cell from a predeclared teacher-local or matched biological neighborhood so that coarse cell-class structure alone cannot pass.

## 6. Candidate family C1 — shared emergent state with relational continuity

Teacher constructs an emergent compact `Z_shared(c)` from rich lawful RNA evidence.

No coordinate is granted biological authority by name. Qualification is by invariants:

- disjoint-view teacher geometry recurrence;
- donor-primary recurrence;
- source replication;
- matched wrong-cell defeat;
- subspace stability under donor-balanced refits;
- no dominant address/support/operator explanation;
- noncollapse.

If axes rotate but the subspace is stable, the scientific object is the stable subspace/block, not individual coordinates.

C1 is allowed only when corrected replay routing retains the broad relational family.

## 7. Candidate family C2 — multiscale relational state

C2 adds an explicit relational training/evaluation protocol around `Z_shared`.

Allowed relational emphasis:
- global/broad donor-local scale-free order;
- mesoscale affinity/rank structure if corrected replay supports it.

Forbidden:
- nearest-third primary mining;
- treating historical nearest-half as the production neighborhood constant;
- post-outcome locality search.

The exact production-scale relation must be defined from the future lawful target-tournament population under a frozen rule. If no lawful non-outcome rule exists, C2 is evaluated as broad/global only.

## 8. Candidate family C3 — q-conditioned measured contextual target

For query `q`, teacher constructs a measured non-q same-cell context `Z_query(c,q)` using a prospectively selected non-q neighborhood/program and independent normalization that excludes q.

Minimum controls:

- q-excluded teacher is primary anchor;
- q-visible teacher is a paired ablation/incumbent only if its q contribution is independently measured;
- q-count-only model;
- query-agnostic shared readout;
- wrong-q readout preserving true-q marginal/bias where possible;
- technical+q baseline;
- matched value/support null.

Unsupported/zero-total target panels abstain. A measured zero within a supported nonempty target remains a legitimate value.

Selection of `N(q)` must use training donors only and must be frozen before heldout target outcomes.

## 9. Candidate family C4 — structured shared + relational + q-context protocol

C4 is the current scientific incumbent architecture, not the winner.

It combines:

- `Z_shared(c)` from C1;
- broad/mesoscale relational constraints from C2;
- measured q-conditioned non-q anchors from C3;
- explicit support/abstention;
- separate biological and measurement uncertainty.

No component may compensate for another component failing its own mandatory gate. For example, strong q-context prediction cannot rescue failed shared-state cross-source recurrence.

C4 is intended to test whether relational continuity supplies a stable global biological coordinate system while measured q-context anchors prevent the representation from becoming an ungrounded self-referential latent.

## 10. Candidate family C5 — optional regulatory/chromatin specialist

C5 is **not** a universal target and is disabled by default.

It may be activated only where independently authenticated regulatory/chromatin support exists under a dedicated contract. Current Stage75/GSE174367 evidence is allowed as:

- soft regulatory prior;
- motif/chromatin-support annotation;
- independent falsifier when circularity is controlled;
- specialist context.

It may not be treated as:

- validated causal regulation;
- a complete eRegulon truth set;
- clean external validation when the same data generated the relation;
- required evidence for RNA-only cells.

A specialist arm must report eligibility and abstention separately.

## 11. Evidence-response and measurement-response qualification

For each candidate state that can be evaluated this way, freeze an evidence ladder such as increasing lawful molecular evidence fractions. Exact fractions and resampling counts require a separate precision/compute freeze before execution and must not be tuned on outcomes.

For a cell `i`:

`C_i(p) = distance(z_i(p), z_i(full_evidence))`

and incremental movement:

`Delta_i(p->p') = distance(z_i(p'), z_i(p))`.

A biological target should show reproducible convergence/stabilization as **biological evidence** increases.

Separately, for the same biological evidence, vary count depth/measurement quality to estimate a measurement-response curve. This separates:

- uncertainty from missing biological evidence;
- uncertainty from noisy measurement of the same evidence.

The tournament must not collapse these into one OOD score.

## 12. Transfer hierarchy

Candidates are evaluated progressively:

1. held-out donor;
2. held-out matrix/operator;
3. held-out dataset/study family;
4. held-out technology where scientifically compatible.

A candidate need not pass a transfer level that the available data cannot validly support, but the level must be reported `NOT_ESTIMABLE`, not silently omitted.

## 13. Coverage accounting

Every candidate reports:

- total eligible cells;
- eligible donors;
- eligible source families/operators;
- eligible queries/addresses;
- abstention reasons;
- support-state distribution;
- source-specific coverage.

Coverage is not maximized by fabricating missing modalities. The intended foundation design is hierarchical:

- universal RNA/shared/relational core;
- conditional q-heads;
- optional specialists.

## 14. Promotion gates

A candidate may be promoted only if all candidate-applicable gates pass:

### G1 — integrity and lineage
Exact bytes/code/input/population/split identities are frozen and clean.

### G2 — nontrivial biological content
Beats B0/B1/B2 and the matched B3 null on donor-primary estimands.

### G3 — correct-cell specificity
Beats matched wrong-cell and, for relational candidates, fine-neighborhood wrong-cell challenges.

### G4 — donor recurrence
No pooled-only success.

### G5 — source replication
No source failure hidden by SEA_AD abundance.

### G6 — teacher information advantage without leakage
Teacher constructs state from richer evidence; student cannot directly or indirectly access withheld target information.

### G7 — evidence convergence
Biological state behavior improves/stabilizes with more lawful biological evidence under the frozen criterion.

### G8 — measurement robustness
Depth/measurement changes do not masquerade as biological state changes.

### G9 — learned-teacher continuity
For learned teachers, rich-state relational geometry recurs across frozen disjoint views before student success is considered.

### G10 — collapse/shortcut resistance
Variance/rank/covariance health is necessary but not sufficient; shortcut baselines remain defeated at selected checkpoint.

### G11 — transfer
Meets the predeclared valid transfer levels available for the candidate.

### G12 — coverage
Meets a prospectively frozen minimum eligible-coverage requirement without forced imputation. The numeric threshold must be derived before execution from corpus/precision requirements, not selected after outcomes.

## 15. Selection logic

- No single scalar leaderboard determines the winner.
- Any mandatory gate failure prevents promotion regardless of average score.
- A simpler target family that passes all gates outranks a richer family with an unqualified component.
- C4 is favored over C1/C2/C3 only if the combination adds donor-primary biological information above its constituent targets without reducing transfer or becoming more shortcut-prone.
- C5 can be accepted as an optional specialist without becoming the foundation target.

If no candidate clears all mandatory gates, terminal is:
`NO_TARGET_WINNER__REDESIGN_BEFORE_PROTECTED_CONFIRMATION`.

## 16. Representation-winner separation

A target-protocol winner does not automatically select a neural architecture or latent width.

After a target protocol wins, representation contestants must be compared under matched target protocol, compute and evidence rights. Representation selection may assess compactness, stability, uncertainty calibration and compute, but it cannot change the biological target after seeing outcomes.

## 17. Training separation

Even a target-protocol and representation winner do not automatically authorize full training. Real training requires its own execution authority, sampling contract, checkpoint selection rule and protected-data firewall.
