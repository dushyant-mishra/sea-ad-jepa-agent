# Unified Synthetic Production Substrate Design

Date: 2026-10-13
Status: **DESIGN FROZEN FOR USER REVIEW — IMPLEMENTATION NOT YET AUTHORIZED**
Successor to: PR #315 V2 design and the qualified PR #328 mechanics/interface fixture.

## 1. Purpose

Build the production synthetic substrate envisioned by PR #315 while preserving the audited boundaries established by PR #328. The production substrate must be ingestible by the same downstream TD / teacher-student interfaces intended for real data, but it must remain candidate-agnostic and must not grant target, representation, Stage4, or training authority.

The production substrate is a successor to, not a relabeling of, the PR #328 fixture. PR #328 remains frozen as qualified mechanics/interface evidence.

## 2. Governing boundaries

1. **Biology before candidates.** C1/C2/C3/C4/C5/C6 may not influence latent generation, sampling, seed choice, interaction design, or observation mechanics.
2. **Controller truth after generation.** Candidate truth is derived from a candidate-neutral world only after biology and observation authorities are frozen.
3. **Donor-primary science.** Donor is the primary biological replication/split unit.
4. **Source/operator are not biological causes.** They may affect acquisition, composition and measurement, but may not directly rewrite latent biology.
5. **Measured zero != structural unmeasurement.** Support is explicit at address level.
6. **No synthetic-to-human authority transfer.** Synthetic rarity/effect/interaction magnitudes are stress geometry, not human prevalence/effect thresholds.
7. **STAT1 50K lineage remains quarantined.** The duplicated-sample_row lineage cannot regain hard empirical calibration authority.
8. **V79 Layer-A is NOT_QUALIFIED.** The single hard failure was the frozen Q08 sparse-positive convergence rule. The production simulator may contain a Q08-like sparse-positive stress family, but must not alter the V79 verdict, copy its human thresholds, or tune to force qualification.
9. **Training remains OFF.** No real TD outcome read, target winner, representation winner, Stage4, or real/synthetic JEPA training is authorized here.

## 3. Architecture

The simulator is split into three trust domains.

### 3.1 Producer domain

Produces candidate-neutral biological truth and molecular expectations:

`donor -> lineage/state -> continuous programs -> regulatory/mechanistic network -> interactions -> context/intervention -> expected molecular state`

The producer owns hidden mechanism state but does not know candidate labels or downstream gate outcomes.

### 3.2 Fit package domain

Receives only lawful measured observations and descriptors:
- measured RNA;
- measured ATAC when a privileged-teacher experiment permits it;
- support masks;
- canonical address registry;
- donor/source/operator metadata permitted by the downstream reader;
- physical context when prospectively allowed;
- query identity/address when prospectively allowed;
- intervention identity only for perturbation experiments.

It must not contain latent factors, expected molecular state, mechanism labels, rare truth, pseudo-rare truth, interaction class, candidate mapping, recoverability labels, counterfactual truth or expected gate outcomes.

### 3.3 Controller domain

Receives sealed producer truth after fit artifacts/rules are frozen and derives:
- biological-program truth;
- relational truth from the biological manifold;
- query-conditioned truth;
- regulatory/mechanistic truth;
- additive/non-additive interaction truth;
- perturbation/counterfactual truth;
- biological-vs-measurement uncertainty diagnostics;
- candidate mappings C1-C6;
- recoverability evaluation targets, but **not preassigned recoverability outcomes**.

For final qualification realization, producer, fit package and controller artifacts must be materialized separately and hash-bound. Controller bytes are not present in the fit package.

## 4. Production population

Default donor partition follows PR #315 unless a newer frozen reader contract supersedes it before realization:
- 104 fit donors;
- 22 validation donors;
- 23 sealed/oracle donors.

The population contains multiple broad lineages/states. Specialist mechanisms are lineage/state eligible, not separate datasets. Common and rare activity arise within the same biological population and may share program bases.

Cell counts per donor/source/lineage are derived from lawful pathology-blind TRAIN distributions under a prospectively frozen compute scaling rule. Donor weighting remains independent of packed cell count.

## 5. Stage81A3 biological mechanics successor

The production generator must recover the scientific mechanics represented by the Stage81A3 lineage rather than merely the simplified PR #328 hierarchical fixture.

Required mechanism families:

1. overlapping lineage/shared programs with positive and negative loadings;
2. multiple continuous biological programs with nontrivial covariance;
3. synthetic causal/regulatory network edges held controller-side;
4. RNA-visible regulatory mechanism;
5. RNA-partially-identifiable mechanism;
6. privileged-modality-dominant mechanism;
7. specialist rare regulatory mechanism using synthetic stress calibration only;
8. explicit interventions/counterfactuals;
9. additive and non-additive interactions.

Historical Stage81A3 source mechanics must be inventoried and hash-bound before implementation. If exact historical code/mechanism definitions cannot be recovered, the missing portion is documented as a new prospectively defined successor rather than reconstructed by guesswork.

## 6. Interaction families

Interaction families are candidate-neutral:
- additive shared + local/query;
- shared x local/query non-additivity;
- additive shared + local + relational;
- higher-order shared x local x context/relational structure.

No latent variable is named for C4/C6 or tuned to make a candidate win. Controller-side mapping later determines which candidate should, in principle, have incremental access to which planted interaction family.

## 7. Query semantics

Queries select lawful biological questions from the same mechanistic system; there is no independent query latent factor.

Each query definition freezes:
- address/module question;
- biological quantity being queried;
- lawful teacher evidence;
- lawful student evidence;
- q-count-only control;
- q-address-only control;
- wrong-q control;
- query-agnostic control;
- teacher query-value intervention;
- target construction that cannot collapse to the queried scalar.

A production query registry is controller-derived from candidate-neutral biology and canonical addresses.

## 8. Relational/context geometry

The biological manifold contains:
- stable broad/global ordering;
- mesoscale relational geometry;
- intentionally weak or unresolved fine-locality structure;
- exact ties where the model should abstain rather than hallucinate order.

Context-responsive and context-insensitive mechanisms coexist. Context controls include true/shuffled/distant views and intrinsic-null programs. Shuffled controls must preserve the existing no-focal/no-adjacent leakage rule.

## 9. Perturbation semantics

Production perturbation arms retain the qualified PR #328 meanings:
- `CONTROL`;
- `ACTIVE_RARE_PROGRAM_PERTURBATION`;
- `MATCHED_PERTURB_NULL`.

Active perturbation acts only on prospectively selected mechanisms. Matched null receives intervention identity/handling but zero target perturbation effect. Common and rare response regimes coexist.

## 10. Canonical address realization

Production qualification uses the canonical 41,238-address namespace unless an exact successor registry is prospectively frozen before realization.

Requirements:
- address identity frozen by hash;
- support measured per modality/source/operator;
- measured zero distinct from unmeasured;
- no positional remapping without address identity proof;
- reader-compatible donor/source/operator/address metadata;
- no gene-semantic use of artifacts classified `GENE_IDENTITY_PROVENANCE_UNRESOLVED`.

Small-dimensional fixtures remain allowed only for tests and do not substitute for production qualification.

## 11. Observation model

Biology is generated first. Acquisition and observation are separate operators.

RNA observation supports:
- structural support;
- detection process;
- positive-value process;
- depth and quadratic-depth effects;
- source/operator observability;
- donor-associated observation structure where prospectively justified;
- repeated draws / count splitting for measurement-uncertainty diagnostics.

Multimodal semantics:
- same-nucleus RNA/ATAC share the same latent cell identity;
- separate-nucleus RNA/ATAC are independently sampled from the same donor/state distribution and expose no synthetic focal-cell pairing.

ATAC contains a mixture of redundant, RNA-predictable stochastic and teacher-private information.

## 12. V79 sparse-positive stress family

Because V79 Layer-A was `NOT_QUALIFIED` solely at `Q08_SPARSE_DETECTION / positive / CONVERGENCE_ALL_FITS_DIAGNOSED`, the production substrate includes a **non-authorizing sparse-positive stress family** with these goals:
- create structurally supported but sparsely positive measurements;
- preserve finite-sample cases in which downstream inference may fail to diagnose;
- provide controller truth for whether failure reflects observation sparsity versus absent biology;
- test lawful abstention/NOT_ESTIMABLE handling.

This family does not inherit V79 human/calibration thresholds and is not tuned so that a downstream estimator passes.

## 13. Uncertainty and recoverability

Biological uncertainty and measurement uncertainty are separate controller diagnostics.

- Biological uncertainty derives from conditional variability in hidden mechanisms given lawful student evidence.
- Measurement uncertainty derives from repeated observation realizations under fixed biological truth.

Recoverability is evaluated downstream from lawful evidence. The generator/controller may expose truth needed to score it but may not preassign `POINT_IDENTIFIABLE`, distributional, teacher-private or NOT_ESTIMABLE outcomes.

## 14. Realization and blinding lifecycle

Use at least two realizations:

### Development realization
Used for implementation tests, reader integration and hostile audits. Truth may be inspectable by the synthetic-lane controller because it is not final qualification evidence.

### Final qualification realization
Generated only after:
- production generator code is frozen;
- canonical address registry is frozen;
- downstream callable executors/rules intended to consume it are frozen;
- runtime is pinned;
- seeds are prospectively frozen.

Final qualification lifecycle:
1. producer generates world + sealed controller bundle;
2. producer emits fit package without controller bundle;
3. fit package is used by downstream rehearsal;
4. outputs are frozen;
5. controller bundle is opened only for scoring;
6. no seed replacement or selective rerun after truth open.

## 15. Custody and reproducibility

Every production receipt binds:
- generator source head and transitive executable file hashes;
- canonical address registry hash;
- observation configuration;
- runtime versions;
- seeds;
- donor split ledger;
- fit package hash;
- sealed controller-bundle hash;
- realization manifest;
- all authority flags false.

Large artifacts may live outside ordinary Git but must have durable off-machine custody, exact size, SHA-256 and immutable locator recorded in the receipt/ledger.

## 16. Hostile tests / recursive self-audit requirements

Implementation proceeds in repeated RED -> GREEN -> audit -> fix cycles. At minimum, each cycle checks:

### Leakage
- no C1-C6 references in producer inputs or latent generation branches;
- no controller fields/paths/hashes in fit package;
- no query/candidate-dependent seed selection;
- no hidden latent or expected-state exposure through reader adapters.

### Biology vs acquisition
- changing source/operator/acquisition changes observed data while fixed biological-world hash remains invariant;
- source sampling may change composition but cannot rewrite a generated cell's latent truth;
- pseudo-rarity occurs with biological rare-program activity absent.

### Multimodal semantics
- same-nucleus pairs share entity identity;
- separate-nucleus data do not expose focal-cell pairing;
- teacher-private ATAC information cannot leak into RNA-only fit views.

### Missingness/support
- measured zero != unmeasured;
- unsupported addresses cannot contribute as zeros;
- source-specific support failures propagate to lawful abstention/NE surfaces.

### Rare / sparse-positive behavior
- no post-hoc forcing of rare positives;
- zero-positive finite realizations are lawful;
- sparse-positive stress is not outcome-tuned;
- V79 thresholds are not reused as human authority.

### Interventions/context
- matched-null perturbation cannot activate target mechanism;
- intervention labels do not change truth absent configured mechanism action;
- shuffled context excludes focal/adjacent leakage;
- context-null mechanisms remain null.

### Splits/custody
- donor-disjoint fit/validation/oracle splits;
- deterministic bytes under the pinned runtime;
- exact source/dependency hashes;
- write-once receipt behavior;
- final controller bundle absent from fit execution domain.

Any audit defect is fixed prospectively and receives a regression test before the next audit cycle.

## 17. Non-goals

This work does not:
- rerun or repair V79 scoring;
- perform real TD adjudication;
- define real-human biological effect thresholds;
- select a target or representation;
- authorize TD60 or Stage4;
- train JEPA on real or synthetic production data.

## 18. Completion criteria

The production synthetic lane is complete only when:
1. Stage81A3-successor mechanistic biology is implemented or any unrecoverable historical mechanics are explicitly re-specified prospectively;
2. full canonical-address production realization is supported;
3. common/rare, context, perturbation, query, relational and multimodal mechanics coexist in one candidate-neutral population;
4. physical producer/fit/controller separation is exercised in a sealed qualification realization;
5. V79-like sparse-positive stress exists without reusing V79 thresholds as human authority;
6. hostile leakage/support/split/custody tests pass under a pinned runtime;
7. repeated recursive self-audits find no unresolved critical/major defect;
8. receipt/ledger freeze the exact non-authorizing production package;
9. terminal authority remains `TARGET_WINNER_NONE__REPRESENTATION_WINNER_NONE__REAL_TRAINING_OFF__STAGE4_NOT_AUTHORIZED`.
