# Lineage-Composite Production Synthetic V3 Cross-Lane Alignment Addendum

Date: 2026-10-14
Status: **PROSPECTIVE DESIGN ALIGNMENT ADDENDUM — USER REVIEW REQUIRED — IMPLEMENTATION OFF**

Base design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-lineage-composite-production-synthetic-design-v2.md`
Controlling amendment: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-lineage-composite-production-synthetic-design-v3-amendment.md`

Purpose: make the production-synthetic design explicitly compatible with the current outcome-blind real-TD/downstream gate contracts without importing human outcomes, thresholds or biological verdicts into the simulator.

## 1. Cross-lane authority boundary

The real-TD/downstream lane and synthetic lane remain separate authority lanes.

Synthetic production must exercise compatible interfaces and known-truth analogues of the real gate mechanics, but it may not use current human candidate outcomes to choose world parameters, thresholds, comparators, support envelopes, evidence ladders or challenge families.

Mechanical compatibility does not create human biological authority.

## 2. G7 biological-evidence ladder compatibility

Production synthetic evidence ladders used to rehearse G7 must satisfy the current frozen real-lane static mechanics:

- evidence changes are `BIOLOGICAL_EVIDENCE_RIGHTS_RESTRICTION` only;
- count/depth thinning is rejected as biological-evidence restriction;
- evidence levels form a strict nested rights ladder with no rights removal;
- each level and evidence-right token is uniquely identified;
- terminal level equals declared full evidence rights exactly;
- fewer than three lawful evidence levels is `NOT_ESTIMABLE`, not an invented ladder;
- the penultimate-to-full contrast is designated prospectively as the deciding terminal contrast;
- strict adjacent monotonicity is not required.

Synthetic development may include richer exploratory ladders, but any same-code-path G7 rehearsal must expose a projection satisfying this contract exactly.

No historical percentage grid becomes human authority.

## 3. G8 measurement-robustness compatibility

Production synthetic measurement-depth worlds must support rehearsal of the current G8 mechanics without turning synthetic tolerances into human tolerances.

For the canonical G8 rehearsal path, support:

- exact nonnegative integer thinning under deterministic namespace-bound randomness;
- disjoint calibration, deciding and null namespaces;
- the currently frozen 25/50/75% depth reference levels where the real executor requires them;
- separate displacement and support-fraction endpoints;
- measured zero retained as measurement;
- unresolved/structurally unsupported entries retained as abstention/support semantics;
- insufficient calibration resolution mapped to `NOT_ESTIMABLE` rather than threshold relaxation.

The simulator may also contain additional depth regimes for stress testing. The human calibration law and tolerance values remain owned by the real lane and must be obtained from lawful human technical calibration, not synthetic values.

## 4. G11/G12 transfer and coverage compatibility

The sealed synthetic package must be able to instantiate an outcome-blind population/evidence/support registry compatible with the current G11/G12 static contract.

Requirements:

- registry is frozen before downstream outcomes;
- transfer dimensions are explicit and drawn from the represented measurement/biology axes;
- supported strata determine `REQUIRED`, `NOT_APPLICABLE_STRUCTURAL` or `BLOCKED_DEPENDENCY` mechanically;
- physically supported frozen strata define the maximum lawful claim envelope;
- zero-supported-stratum cases remain `NOT_ESTIMABLE`;
- support-limited specialist cases cannot be promoted to foundation-wide claims;
- post-hoc scope shrinkage after seeing outcomes is forbidden.

This aligns with V3's separate biological-novelty and measurement-domain axes while preserving the real lane's non-compensation and claim-envelope logic.

## 5. G6 teacher/student evidence-right compatibility

The synthetic teacher/student surface must preserve distinct lawful evidence-right matrices for teacher and student so that the current G6 finite-probe machinery can be rehearsed without hidden controller truth.

Requirements:

- teacher evidence consists only of lawful measured observations;
- student evidence is a prospectively restricted lawful subset/alternative view;
- hidden synthetic factors and expected molecular state are controller-only and never teacher inputs;
- teacher and student search/model rights used in a matched diagnostic must be identical except for the evidence-right difference being tested;
- negative synthetic results under a finite diagnostic must not be labeled information-theoretic impossibility.

## 6. R1 relational compatibility

Production relational worlds must support the real lane's scale-free triplet-order family without inheriting historical human thresholds.

Where a synthetic R1 rehearsal is declared:

- relation identity uses repaired 41,238-address-bound members;
- unsupported or collision-unresolved required members cause abstention rather than imputation;
- measured zero remains measurement;
- relation-label nulls preserve donor/source/support structure;
- source requirements are noncompensating when the downstream contract marks them required;
- recurrence/adverse-null/substantive thresholds remain prospective and benchmark-specific, never copied from historical human cutoffs.

## 7. TargetPortfolio compatibility

Synthetic downstream rehearsal must preserve the corrected portfolio semantics:

- C1/C2/C3 can coexist independently;
- C6/C4 are optional fused/composite blocks;
- composite success cannot rescue failed constituents;
- composite failure cannot delete independently qualified constituents;
- C5 remains specialist-only;
- `NOT_ESTIMABLE` remains explicit and unresolved.

The production simulator may test these mechanics with controller-known truth but cannot preassign a real human target winner.

## 8. Additional cross-lane hostile tests

Add the following hostile checks to the V3 suite:

1. count thinning falsely accepted as G7 biological evidence;
2. non-nested G7 evidence rights accepted;
3. terminal G7 level not equal to full rights;
4. synthetic G8 tolerance imported as human calibration;
5. G8 support collapse hidden by displacement-only scoring;
6. G11/G12 claim envelope shrunk after outcome visibility;
7. unsupported transfer stratum silently dropped instead of explicit status;
8. hidden controller truth exposed to G6 teacher input;
9. R1 unsupported member imputed to obtain a relation;
10. composite TargetPortfolio block rescuing a failed constituent.

## 9. Stop conditions

Stop if cross-lane compatibility would require:

- importing current human outcomes into synthetic generation;
- importing synthetic thresholds as human calibration;
- weakening a real-lane static contract for convenience;
- changing the frozen V79/V80 benchmark packages;
- turning mechanical compatibility into target/representation/training authority.

Authority remains:

`TARGET_WINNER_NONE__REPRESENTATION_WINNER_NONE__REAL_TRAINING_OFF__STAGE4_NOT_AUTHORIZED`
