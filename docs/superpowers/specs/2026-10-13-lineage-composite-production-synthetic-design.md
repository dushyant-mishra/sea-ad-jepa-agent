## 1. Purpose

Build the final production synthetic substrate for the JEPA project as a **lineage-composite successor**, not as a rewrite of one historical simulator and not as a candidate-shaped benchmark.

The substrate must provide one candidate-neutral hidden biological population, project that population into the lawful observation modalities used by the downstream pipeline, and support blinded qualification of target-discovery, teacher/student, recoverability, uncertainty, representation, and downstream execution mechanics without granting human biological authority.

The key correction relative to prior design is architectural ancestry. Production authority is not inherited from a single sequence such as `Stage81A3 -> V79`. The production substrate must compose the latest still-valid lessons from several independently evolved lineages while preserving each lineage's negative results and supersessions.

The intended output is therefore:

`candidate-neutral biological truth`

`-> expected molecular state`

`-> acquisition / population-selection process`

`-> modality-specific observation process`

`-> canonical fit-side reader surface`

with hidden controller-only truth and post-fit candidate mappings derived only after biological world construction.

The final design must remain compatible with the same downstream code paths intended for real data. Synthetic-only shortcut learners or synthetic-only target logic do not qualify the real pipeline.

- target winner;
- representation winner;
- architecture winner;
- EMA timescale;
- TD60 authority;

`TARGET_WINNER_NONE__REPRESENTATION_WINNER_NONE__REAL_TRAINING_OFF__STAGE4_NOT_AUTHORIZED`

Synthetic production work may qualify software, interfaces, measurement mechanics, test behavior, falsifiers, support semantics, blinding, and known-truth recovery. It may falsify a proposed method when the method fails on applicable known truth. It may not rescue a real-data FAIL or NOT_ESTIMABLE result and may not promote human numerical thresholds unless a separate real-data authority explicitly does so.

## 3. Composite ancestry and supersession map

The production substrate must explicitly bind and classify the following lineages.

### 3.1 Stage81A3 / Stage81A3R — biological/mechanistic ancestor plus corrected support/address semantics

Reusable scientific mechanics:

- latent biological factors and overlapping program structure;
- biological mechanisms upstream of measurement;
- noise-free expected molecular state separated from sequencing realizations;
- interventions/counterfactuals at the biological mechanism layer;
- donor/cell organization;
- canonical-address/support lineage;
- later corrected TRAIN support/address semantics.

Production correction:

Stage81A3 is the mechanistic ancestor; Stage81A3R and its corrected support lineage are the later source for support/address lessons. Historical A3/A3R realized caches remain historical custody and are not automatically the newest empirical calibration substrate when later repaired S174 authority exists.

Support is not binary. Production must distinguish at least:

1. `STRUCTURALLY_UNMEASURED`;
2. `MEASURED_SCALAR`;
3. `MEASURED_COLLISION_UNRESOLVED`.

A measured zero is a lawful scalar measurement. It is not structural absence. A collision-unresolved measurement may have assay evidence but no lawful single scalar at the canonical-address level. Fit-side readers and scorers must preserve these distinctions.

### 3.2 V72 -> V75 — coupled ecosystem, sharding, population allocation, calibration consumption, measurement architecture

This lineage is required production ancestry.

Reusable components:

- one hidden truth projected into several dataset/modal views;
- deterministic cell-identity-keyed sharding;
- shard-size invariance;
- same hidden biology underlying RNA, paired multiome, separate-nucleus modality views, perturbation-like data and spatial-like limited panels;
- source composition and scale planning;
- source -> operator -> donor/group allocation that preserves low-support operators;
- empirical QC/depth/support distributions consumed by the observer rather than merely stored in metadata;
- fragment generation and byte/multiplicity reconciliation;
- bounded resource estimates and deterministic large-scale shard planning;
- protected-data firewalls;
- adversarial checkpoint twins.

V75 physically qualified the 100K measurement architecture within its declared scope. That does not authorize 500K or full-scale training, but its qualified measurement/population mechanics supersede older engineering placeholders for the production observer.

Required adversarial checkpoint/world roles inherited from V72:

- healthy;
- representation collapse;
- source shortcut;
- donor shortcut;
- private-information leak;
- overconfident unrecoverable prediction;
- corrupted manifest / axis.

These are test roles, not biological programs.

## 4. Production architecture

### 4.1 One candidate-neutral master biological population

Generate one master hidden population. All modality-specific views are projections of that population or lawful independently sampled views from its donor/state distributions.

For cell/nucleus `i` from donor `d`:

`B_i = f(lineage_i, donor_state_d, broad_programs_i, specialist_programs_i, regulatory_mechanisms_i, context_i, intervention_i, interaction_terms_i)`

Requirements:

- overlapping mechanisms;
- positive and negative gene loadings;
- non-orthogonal factors;
- broad/common and rare/tail activity in the same population;
- donor recurrence;
- cell-state dependence;
- multiple intrinsic dimensions;
- additive and non-additive mechanism families;
- no candidate names in generator inputs;
- no source/operator direct causal input to latent biology.

### 4.2 Biological mechanism families

At minimum include:

1. broad lineage/state biology;
2. shared continuous programs;
3. rare specialist programs;
4. RNA-visible regulatory programs;
5. RNA-partially-identifiable regulatory programs;
6. privileged-modality-dominant mechanisms;
7. context-responsive mechanisms;
8. context-null mechanisms;
9. perturbable mechanisms with matched null interventions;
10. additive interaction families;
11. non-additive shared x query/local interaction families;
12. higher-order structured interaction families;
13. biological nulls and weak-effect families;
14. sparse-positive families that stress inference/support.

Synthetic causal edges/mechanisms are controller truth only.

### 4.3 Broad class is part of truth

Lineage and broad cell state are biological variables upstream of expected molecular state. They may influence program eligibility, baseline expression, program loading and state transitions.

The acquisition layer may subsequently sample lineages/states non-uniformly, creating source-biology association without making source a biological cause.

### 4.4 Rare/common continuity

Rarity is an activity/frequency regime inside the same mechanism family whenever biologically coherent. Avoid separate 'rare datasets'.

Use synthetic stress presets unless an independently qualified human calibration exists. Quarantined STAT1/50K moments remain non-authority. Any later empirical calibration must be separately provenance-bound and may not silently mutate the base world after qualification rules are frozen.

## 5. Expected molecular state

Hidden biology generates a noise-free expected molecular state before acquisition/measurement effects.

This object:

- is controller-visible;
- supports biological counterfactuals;
- supports measurement-twin generation under fixed biology;
- is not normally fit-side data;
- cannot be used as a teacher shortcut unless an explicit experiment prospectively defines an oracle/mechanics-only teacher, which then cannot count as lawful production teacher qualification.

## 6. Population and acquisition layer

### 6.1 Donor hierarchy

Use donor-primary scientific mass. Cell count cannot redefine donor weight.

Synthetic donors must support:

- fit donors;
- validation donors;
- sealed/oracle qualification donors;
- held-out new-donor draws;
- same-donor repeat observations where required.

Default split may mirror the current reader partition only if the implementation binds the exact current reader authority. Otherwise the split must be frozen prospectively from the then-current authority before realization.

### 6.2 Source / operator / donor allocation

Population allocation follows the later V74/V75 lesson:

`source -> feasible operator -> donor/group -> cells`

rather than an allocation rule that can round low-support operators to zero.

Requirements:

- source totals exact under the chosen production scale;
- every feasible required operator retains nonzero support at qualified stress scales;
- donor imbalance may be realistic but donor-primary estimands remain protected;
- deterministic allocation under a frozen seed/identity contract;
- shard boundaries do not change scientific assignments.

### 6.3 Acquisition is not biological causation

Source/operator may affect:

- which cells/states are sampled;
- depth/support;
- detection/positive observation parameters;
- modality availability;
- technical pseudo-rarity;
- measurement noise.

Source/operator may not directly change latent biological mechanism values except through a prospectively explicit acquisition-selection mechanism that samples different biological states.

## 7. Canonical address and support contract

### 7.1 Production address vocabulary

The final qualification realization must bind the exact modern canonical address registry intended by the production reader. Current design target is the project 41,238-address namespace, but implementation must fail closed if the current runtime authority points to a different successor registry.

No silent remapping.

### 7.2 Support states

Minimum support-state algebra:

- `STRUCTURALLY_UNMEASURED`;
- `MEASURED_SCALAR`;
- `MEASURED_COLLISION_UNRESOLVED`.

Additional modality-specific support states may be added only if their semantics are explicit and losslessly project to the three-state base distinction.

Rules:

- measured zero remains `MEASURED_SCALAR`;
- unsupported payload bytes are poison-insensitive;
- collision-unresolved cannot be coerced to zero;
- fit-side masks and readers preserve support state;
- scorers distinguish absence of support from observed zero from unresolved scalarization.

## 8. Observation architecture

### 8.1 RNA

Use a two-part count/continuous observation family compatible with V79 lessons:

- structural support;
- detection probability;
- positive magnitude conditional on detection;
- depth and nonlinear depth dependence;
- source/acquisition effect;
- donor-within-source observation effect where justified;
- optional operator perturbation stress;
- repeated observations under fixed biology where measurement uncertainty is evaluated.

The production observer is a modern successor, not a rewrite of Q00-Q11.

### 8.2 Same-nucleus RNA + ATAC

Same hidden nucleus generates both modalities.

The paired view must contain a mixture of:

- redundant information;
- RNA-predictable but stochastic privileged increment;
- teacher-private privileged increment.

ATAC/regulatory outputs must preserve feature/address custody and may additionally generate raw fragment views if required by downstream Route-B/SCENIC+-style tests.

### 8.3 Raw fragment view

Where the downstream path consumes fragments, generate fragments from the same hidden ATAC observation process.

Required mechanics:

- barcode identity bound to cell identity;
- fragment file content hash bound in receipts;
- multiplicity reconciliation;
- duplicate/conflicting barcode detection before dictionary collapse;
- zero-region/no-consensus cases fail closed with explicit status;
- no fragment-only hidden truth fields.

### 8.4 Separate-nucleus multimodal view

RNA and ATAC cells are independently sampled from the same donor x lineage/state distribution.

No synthetic one-to-one cell pairing is exposed. Evaluation is donor, donor x state, or another prospectively lawful aggregate level.

### 8.5 Limited-panel spatial/context view

Generate coordinates/neighborhoods from the same master biological population and acquisition process.

Required controls:

- true context;
- shuffled context;
- distant context;
- intrinsic context-null biology;
- coverage-limited panels;
- support/segmentation stress;
- no focal-adjacent shuffled-neighbor leakage.

Spatial absence of a program may be NOT_ESTIMABLE when the panel does not lawfully observe it.

### 8.6 Perturbation view

Perturb mechanisms upstream of expected molecular state.

Prospectively bind:

- `CONTROL`;
- `ACTIVE_MECHANISM_PERTURBATION`;
- `MATCHED_PERTURB_NULL`.

Matched null cannot activate the targeted mechanism. Perturbation metadata must not leak hidden response labels.

### 8.7 Technical pseudo-rarity

Pseudo-rare observations arise from acquisition/support/depth/noise while the relevant biological rare mechanism is absent.

True rare and pseudo-rare cells coexist in one observed population.

## 9. Query semantics and leakage firewall

Queries select questions about the same underlying biological system. The generator does not create an independent query latent solely to reward query-conditioned methods.

For each query case define controller-side:

- queried biological quantity;
- lawful teacher evidence;
- lawful student evidence;
- whether useful increment beyond query identity/value exists;
- whether that increment is deterministic, stochastic, teacher-private, null, or NOT_ESTIMABLE.

Fit-side controls must include:

- q-value-only;
- q-address-only;
- wrong-q;
- query-agnostic capacity-matched model;
- teacher query-value intervention;
- query exchangeability;
- normalization/QC descendant leakage audit;
- remaining-RNA necessity;
- null query cases.

If a construction is intended to be value-blind, the query scalar must be excluded not only from its visible token but also from normalization, QC and any derived quantity supplied to other tokens.

No synthetic fixture outcome selects the final human teacher target.

## 10. Regulatory specificity challenge family

Regulatory challenges must be designed to detect false positives produced by nuisance structure.

Required matched sets:

- genuine transported regulatory mechanism;
- matched pseudo-cis nuisance;
- locus/local-architecture matched null;
- under-matched positive-control nuisance showing the test can be fooled;
- semantic/annotation twin;
- mixed-sign true mechanism;
- sparse true mechanism;
- weak true mechanism;
- donor-limited / underpowered mechanism;
- privileged-only mechanism;
- RNA-recoverable mechanism.

Qualification logic must be allowed to return NOT_ESTIMABLE. More decoys or donors may not be added after outcomes are read merely to obtain a PASS.

## 11. Relational geometry

Relational truth is induced by the underlying biological manifold, not planted as a candidate-named coordinate.

Include:

- broad/global geometry;
- mesoscale geometry;
- weak or absent hard nearest-local recurrence in selected worlds;
- ties;
- source-visibility counterfactuals under identical biology;
- donor-restricted cases;
- underpowered cases;
- technical twins.

Known synthetic TD qualification packages remain separate test-of-test authorities. Production worlds should reuse their blinding/role-control lessons, not copy their candidate-shaped geometry as production biology.

## 12. Additive / non-additive mechanism families

To test composite candidate increment without planting candidate winners, include generic mechanism families:

- additive shared + query/local;
- non-additive shared x query/local;
- additive shared + relational + query/local;
- higher-order structured interactions;
- null/no-increment families.

Controller-side candidate truth is derived after generation. A composite PASS must never rescue a failed constituent or retroactively grant constituent permission.

## 13. Recoverability and uncertainty

### 13.1 Recoverability

Recoverability is not preassigned fit-side metadata.

Controller may estimate classes after realization from the joint distribution:

- highly recoverable;
- predictable but stochastic;
- teacher-private;
- NOT_ESTIMABLE.

Fit-side code sees only lawful evidence/support.

### 13.2 Biological uncertainty

Estimate from conditional variability of hidden biological quantities given lawful student evidence.

Do not plant arbitrary uncertainty coordinates solely to create an uncertainty task.

### 13.3 Measurement uncertainty

Estimate using repeated measurement draws, count splitting, depth perturbation or other observation-only variation under fixed hidden biology.

Biological and measurement uncertainty must remain distinct in both truth and scoring.

## 14. Development versus sealed qualification realizations

There is one frozen production generator but at least two realization classes.

### 14.1 Development realization

Purpose:

- generator debugging;
- test development;
- hostile controls;
- interface rehearsal;
- resource profiling.

Truth may be available to the development controller.

### 14.2 Sealed qualification realization

Generated from an independently frozen seed/root after rules are frozen.

Required physical trust domains:

1. **producer domain** — creates world plus controller and fit packages;
2. **fit domain** — receives only blinded fit-side transport;
3. **controller domain** — receives controller mapping/truth only after fit outputs freeze.

The fit package must not contain:

- latent factors;
- expected molecular state;
- candidate mappings;
- challenge semantic roles where those would reveal the answer;
- truth labels;
- expected terminal status;
- recoverability labels;
- hidden program identity;
- precomputed truth-aware distances/statistics.

Controller unblinding happens once after outputs freeze. Selective seed replacement or selective rerun after truth read is forbidden.

## 22. Implementation decomposition

The architectural scope is large enough that implementation must be decomposed into reviewable units even if they remain on one successor lineage.

Recommended sequence:

Machine-encode the ancestry table, negative priors, exact source heads/blobs, calibration-status categories and forbidden promotions.

Implement Stage81A3/A3R-inspired mechanistic biology plus broad-class, rare/common, regulatory and interaction families while preserving candidate-neutrality.

### Unit 3 — population/acquisition/scaling successor

Implement V72-V75-style deterministic sharding, source/operator/donor allocation, donor-primary split semantics and scale/resource guards.

### Unit 4 — canonical support/address successor

### Unit 5 — observation/modalities successor

### Unit 6 — query/regulatory hostile families

Bind the real downstream reader/tournament/teacher-student/portfolio interfaces without synthetic-only shortcuts.

### Unit 9 — production-scale rehearsal and freeze

After all prior units and resource gate pass, generate/freeze the production qualification package under separate execution authority.

## 23. Stop conditions

Implementation stops and returns to controller if any of the following occurs:

- current canonical address authority cannot be reconciled without semantic remapping;
- required lineage source identity cannot be proven;
- a historical negative result would need to be relabeled as authority to proceed;
- source/operator leaks directly into latent biology outside the acquisition-selection contract;
- controller truth becomes reachable from fit-side imports/transport;
- query scalar leaks through normalization/QC in a supposedly value-blind case;
