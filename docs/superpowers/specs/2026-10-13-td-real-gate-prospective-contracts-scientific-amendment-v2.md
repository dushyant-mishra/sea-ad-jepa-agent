## 1. Core correction: statistical direction is not biological magnitude

The donor-primary exact sign-test framework remains a qualified reusable inference component, but it does **not** by itself establish a biologically meaningful magnitude, stability margin, information increment, or noninferiority claim.

Therefore gates are separated into two classes.

These may lawfully use the current donor-primary directional framework when their comparator is already frozen and their claim is limited to directional defeat, recurrence of a primary endpoint, provenance, or structural compliance:

- `G3_CORRECT_CELL_SPECIFICITY` — B3 and applicable B4 defeat only;
- `G4_DONOR_RECURRENCE` — recurrence of the candidate-primary predictive endpoint only;
- `G5_SOURCE_REPLICATION` — source-stratified recurrence with no pooled rescue;
- `G10_COLLAPSE_SHORTCUT_RESISTANCE` — mandatory frozen falsifier defeat;
- `S3_IDENTITY_SUPPORT_BASELINE_DEFEAT` — baseline defeat only, not substantive shared-state magnitude;
- `R3_FINE_NEIGHBOR_WRONG_CELL_DEFEAT` — falsifier defeat where lawfully estimable;
- `Q4_Q_COUNT_ONLY_DEFEAT`;

This amendment does not itself authorize implementation or execution of these gates; it only states that their scientific decision form is not blocked by an unresolved biological magnitude margin, subject to their existing structural dependencies and exact frozen comparator ledgers.

## 2. Recurrence family: exact two-level estimand is not yet frozen

The parent design specifies a rotation/scale-invariant observed recurrence statistic and a donor-preserving null, but it does not prospectively select the exact scalar comparison between the observed donor statistic and the null distribution.

The unresolved choice includes, but is not limited to:
- null mean;
- null median;
- a predeclared null quantile;
- an exact within-donor permutation p-value transformed into a donor-level statistic;
- another independently justified null-standardized quantity.

Those choices are not interchangeable. Therefore `G7`, `G9`, `S1`, `S2`, and `R1` remain blocked. No executor is permitted to choose the null summary after seeing candidate values.

A future amendment must freeze the complete chain:

`observed donor statistic -> exact null statistic/summary -> donor contrast -> across-donor decision rule -> substantive stability criterion`.

## 3. G7: strict adjacent monotonicity is withdrawn

The prior V1 self-audit amendment required every adjacent instability step to shrink. That rule is superseded.

The frozen historical scientific requirement is stabilization as lawful biological evidence increases, not strict stepwise monotonicity.

Until an outcome-blind evidence ladder and a terminal/plateau stability criterion are independently justified, `G7_EVIDENCE_CONVERGENCE` remains `BLOCKED_PROSPECTIVE_RULE_REQUIRED`.

No adjacent-step monotonicity failure may be interpreted as biological failure under the current contract.

## 4. G8: preserve mechanics, withdraw invented zero-margin rule

The previously frozen measurement mechanics remain binding:

`25% -> 50% -> 75% -> 100%`

with same-cell complementary raw-count splitting and/or nested raw-count thinning, fixed support universe, measured-zero preservation, deterministic seed namespace, and no relabeling as biological replication.

The prior V1 amendment's decision contrast

`D(A_d,B_d) - mean(D(full,A_d), D(full,B_d))`

is withdrawn as an unauthorized scientific margin. The shared derivation of `full`, `A_d`, and `B_d` means zero is not established as a valid biological noninferiority boundary.

`G8_MEASUREMENT_ROBUSTNESS` remains `BLOCKED_PROSPECTIVE_RULE_REQUIRED` until either:

1. the prospectively authorized empirical measurement-reference distribution is generated and a later outcome-blind margin is frozen without candidate-performance tuning; or
2. a mathematically justified outcome-blind comparison law is independently frozen before candidate values are opened.

The measurement mechanics themselves remain frozen and reusable; the biological decision rule does not.

## 5. G6: matched predictor protocol is not yet a fixed estimand

The phrase `strongest matched predictor restricted to student evidence` is insufficiently specified.

Before `G6_TEACHER_INFORMATION_ADVANTAGE` can be executable, a prospective comparator protocol must freeze at minimum:

- model family or finite predeclared family;
- optimization budget;
- tuning/hyperparameter rights;
- training/validation split structure;
- model-selection rule;
- matched-compute definition;
- stopping/failure handling;
- exact evidence firewall.

Until that protocol is frozen, G6 remains `BLOCKED_PROSPECTIVE_RULE_REQUIRED`.

## 6. G12: biological coverage is not statistical resolvability

The prior V1 amendment is superseded where it defined minimum eligible coverage by the donor count needed for the significance test.

Inferential resolution is necessary but cannot define biological claim coverage.

Before `G12_COVERAGE_ABSTENTION` is executable, the project must prospectively freeze either:

- an explicit biological support/coverage requirement for each claimed stratum; or
- a narrower candidate claim whose stated scope is exactly the support represented.

In all cases:
- structural unsupportedness remains abstention;
- imputation remains forbidden;
- measured zero remains valid measurement;
- inferential resolution remains a separate necessary condition;
- a small selected donor subset cannot silently stand for an unsupported broader claim.

Until the claim-scope/coverage contract is frozen, G12 remains `BLOCKED_PROSPECTIVE_RULE_REQUIRED`.

## 7. G11: transfer applicability map remains prospectively unresolved

`G11_TRANSFER` cannot use a generic runtime rule such as “test whichever transfer dimensions exist.”

Before implementation, an outcome-blind candidate-by-transfer-stratum applicability matrix must be frozen for:

- donor;
- source;
- operator/matrix;
- study family;
- technology;
- any explicitly supported specialist dimension.

Each candidate/stratum entry must be one of:

- `REQUIRED`;
- `NOT_APPLICABLE_STRUCTURAL`;
- `BLOCKED_DEPENDENCY`.

The map must be based on the frozen population/metadata/evidence-rights contract, not candidate performance.

Until that exact map exists, `G11_TRANSFER` remains `BLOCKED_PROSPECTIVE_RULE_REQUIRED`.

## 8. C4 comparator conflict is prospectively resolved in favor of the later C6 factorial freeze

The earlier multitarget V2 language used “strongest qualified constituent.” The later prospectively frozen C6 design explicitly defines the clean factorial relational question as:

`C4 versus C6: does relational structure add value after shared + query context already exist?`

For future execution, this later specific factorial contract controls the C4 increment question.

Therefore:

- `C4_INCREMENT_OVER_CONSTITUENTS` must compare C4 against independently qualified C6 under matched evidence;
- C6 must independently satisfy its own qualification before this C4 increment is estimable;
- if C6 is not independently qualified, the C4 increment is unresolved/`NOT_ESTIMABLE` and C4 cannot use a different comparator opportunistically;
- C4 failure or NE never deletes independently qualified C1/C2/C3/C6 blocks;
- no composite PASS may rescue a constituent failure.

This amendment does not authorize C6 or C4 execution. Both increment gates remain blocked on a substantive magnitude criterion even after the comparator identity is fixed.

## 9. G2, R4, C6 and C4 require meaningful increment, not merely sign

For these gates, a donor-recurrent positive difference of arbitrarily small magnitude is not enough to establish the stated scientific claim.

A future prospective amendment must bind an independently justified effect/noise-relative criterion before these gates can be implemented. Permissible sources include an independent measurement/noise reference or another scientifically defended pre-outcome calibration. Historical candidate outcomes and synthetic human-effect analogies are not permissible sources.

Until then these gates remain blocked.

## 10. Cross-candidate multiplicity and selection boundary

Gate-local familywise testing in PR #338 is hereby classified as **qualification screening only**. It does not control a cross-candidate winner-selection family and cannot by itself authorize a winner.

No candidate may be selected by counting gate PASSes, aggregate p-values, rank sums, or choosing the most favorable survivor.

If multiple candidates survive gate qualification, the already separate protected head-to-head / convergence lane remains mandatory. Before any protected head-to-head or confirmation values are opened, that lane must prospectively freeze its own comparison family and multiplicity/decision rule.

Thus the current PR #338 gate-local alpha does not claim tournament-wide familywise winner control.
