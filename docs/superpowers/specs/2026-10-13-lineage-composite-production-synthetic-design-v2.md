## 1. Objective

Build the final production synthetic substrate as a **lineage-composite successor**: one candidate-neutral hidden biological population, one explicit bridge to expected molecular state, one acquisition/measurement layer, multiple lawful modality views, a canonical fit-side reader surface, and a physically separate controller truth surface.

The substrate exists to qualify the actual downstream JEPA target-discovery and teacher/student pipeline against sealed known truth. It is not itself human biology authority and cannot select a target, representation, architecture, EMA timescale, Stage4 plan or training plan.

Binding terminal:

`TARGET_WINNER_NONE__REPRESENTATION_WINNER_NONE__REAL_TRAINING_OFF__STAGE4_NOT_AUTHORIZED`

## 4. Master biological population

`B_i = f(lineage_i, donor_state_d, shared_programs_i, specialist_programs_i, regulatory_mechanisms_i, context_i, intervention_i, interaction_terms_i)`

Requirements:

- overlapping/non-orthogonal mechanisms;

- additive and non-additive mechanisms;
- biological nulls and weak effects;
- sparse-positive mechanisms;
- no C1/C2/C3/C6/C4/C5 names in generator inputs;
- no direct source/operator causal input to latent biology.

Required mechanism families:

1. broad lineage/state;

3. rare specialist programs;

5. partially RNA-identifiable regulatory mechanisms;

12. additive shared + relational + query families;

Rarity is a regime within the same population, not a separate rare dataset.

## 8. Observation/modalities

### RNA

Use a V79-compatible two-part observation family with support, detection, positive magnitude, nonlinear depth, acquisition/source effects, donor-within-source structure and optional operator stress.

### Same-nucleus RNA+ATAC

Both modalities arise from the same hidden nucleus. Include redundant, stochastic-recoverable and teacher-private privileged information.

### Raw fragments

When downstream Route-B/SCENIC+-style paths are exercised:

- bind barcode to cell identity;
- hash-bind fragment content;
- reconcile multiplicity;
- detect duplicate/conflicting barcode rows before collapse;
- fail closed on zero-consensus cases;
- expose no hidden truth.

### Separate-nucleus multimodal

RNA and ATAC are independently sampled from the same donor x state distribution. No false one-to-one cell pairing.

### Spatial/context

Provide true/shuffled/distant context, intrinsic context-null biology, limited-panel support and segmentation/coverage stress.

### Perturbation

Prospectively bind:

- `CONTROL`;
- `ACTIVE_MECHANISM_PERTURBATION`;
- `MATCHED_PERTURB_NULL`.

Matched null cannot activate the target mechanism.

### Technical pseudo-rarity

Observation-side rarity occurs without the corresponding biological rare program.

## 12. Recoverability and uncertainty

Controller-side recoverability classes may be derived after realization:

- highly recoverable;
- predictable but stochastic;
- teacher-private;
- NOT_ESTIMABLE.

Biological uncertainty comes from conditional variability of hidden biology given lawful student evidence.

Measurement uncertainty comes from observation-only variation under fixed hidden biology.

Keep them separate in truth and scoring.
