## 1. Purpose

Build one production synthetic biological substrate that can be consumed by the same downstream target-discovery and teacher/student pipeline intended for real data, while preserving exact controller truth.

This design integrates rather than replaces three existing project lineages:

1. **Stage81A3 biological synthetic mechanics** — latent biological factors, a synthetic causal/mechanistic network, gene modules, noise-free expected molecular state, paired sequencing realizations and counterfactual interventions.
2. **V79 Q00–Q11** — immutable observation/statistical qualification worlds for depth, class, source, donor, structural support, sparse detection, tails, S174 imbalance and omitted operator effects.
3. **PR #311** — donor-calibrated rare-program, technical pseudo-rarity, same-vs-separate nucleus, context and perturbation component work.

The output is **not a rare dataset** and is **not a new candidate-shaped universe**. It is one candidate-agnostic biological population over which the real TD candidate system can be rehearsed.

Primary objective:

> Before opening corresponding real biological outcomes, prove that the actual downstream candidate tournament, target-portfolio convergence, rich-teacher/partial-RNA decomposition, student architecture, uncertainty/support handling, transport and learned-teacher continuity behave correctly against a hidden biological truth that they did not receive during fitting.

## 2. Authority and historical boundaries

### 2.1 Q00–Q11

Q00–Q11 remain byte-immutable and separately governed. They are not modified, regenerated or reinterpreted as the new biological dataset.

Their role is Layer 0 qualification of measurement/statistical machinery. The unified simulator may reuse their validated scientific lessons and compatible observation primitives, but a PASS in the unified simulator cannot rewrite any Q00–Q11 outcome.

### 2.2 Stage81A3 biological simulator

The historical `conditional_predictability` fixture already established the important synthetic separation:

`true biological factors -> expected molecular state -> independent sequencing observations`

and includes a synthetic mechanistic network plus interventions.

V2 treats this as the conceptual biological ancestor of the production simulator. Historical 4,096-gene mechanics fixtures remain immutable evidence; the production substrate is a modernized successor, not a silent mutation of old evidence.

### 2.3 PR #311 rare/multimodal work

PR #311 V1/V2/V3 results remain historical **component-level synthetic geometry/engineering evidence**. They are not final qualification of the V2 unified substrate.

Reusable pieces include:
- TRAIN-calibrated donor recurrence/tail behavior;
- STAT1-like multigene regulatory prototype;
- technical pseudo-rarity;
- source-specific observability/noise lessons;
- same-nucleus versus separate-nucleus semantics;
- evidence/depth ladders;
- spatial true/shuffled/distant controls including the repaired leakage guard;
- perturbation actual/null controls;
- RNA-recoverable versus teacher-private challenge logic.

## 3. Core design principle: biology first, candidates second

The generator must not plant `C1`, `C2`, `C3`, `C4`, `C5` or `C6` as latent coordinates.

Instead it generates a candidate-agnostic biological system. Candidate-specific truth objects are derived later by a controller-only truth adapter.

### 3.1 Underlying biological state

For cell `i` from donor `d`:

`B_i = f(lineage_i, donor_state_d, continuous_programs_i, regulatory_mechanisms_i, context_i, intervention_i, interactions_i)`

where:
- programs may overlap in genes;
- factors need not be orthogonal;
- some mechanisms are correlated or causally linked;
- some are independent nuisance/biological axes;
- activity spans common through rare tails;
- eligibility depends on lineage/state where appropriate.

The biological state is converted to a noise-free expected molecular state before any observation process is applied.

### 3.2 Candidate truth is a derived controller view

The controller derives TD truth from the same `B_i`:

- **C1/shared truth:** global/shared biological subspace or sufficient-statistic family induced by the underlying programs.
- **C2/relational truth:** anchored orderings/distances induced by the same biological manifold; no independent `Z_rel` factor is created merely to reward C2.
- **C3/query truth:** query-conditioned information obtained by selecting a biological question/address/module within the same program network; no independent `Z_query` factor is created.
- **C5/regulatory truth:** mechanistic/regulatory components that are visible to RNA and/or privileged modalities.
- **C6/C4 increment truth:** arises only when generic non-additive biological interactions exist beyond additive/shared/query/relational constituents.

Candidate names never appear in the fit-side biological generator inputs.

## 4. Production population

### 4.1 Donor split

Default production split mirrors the current 149 TRAIN-donor reader partition:
- 104 synthetic fit donors;
- 22 synthetic validation donors;
- 23 synthetic oracle/sealed qualification donors.

Any departure requires a separate prospective authority.

Scientific mass is donor-primary. Raw cell count does not set donor weight.

### 4.2 Multi-lineage population

The simulator includes ordinary broad lineages/cell states rather than a microglia-only universe.

Lineage proportions, donor composition and source sampling are calibrated only from lawful pathology-blind TRAIN information.

Specialist program eligibility is explicit. Example: a STAT1-like rare regulatory program may be eligible in a microglia-like lineage/state while other cells retain ordinary/shared biology.

### 4.3 Continuous and rare activity in the same programs

Rarity is an activity/frequency regime, not a separate dataset.

For eligible programs, donor and cell activity distributions may include:
- zero/inactive mass where biologically appropriate;
- low continuous activity;
- intermediate activity;
- common high activity;
- rare high-activity tails.

Rare and common realizations share the same underlying program basis unless a separate biological mechanism is explicitly planted prospectively.

The STAT1 TRAIN calibration may inform one specialist program's donor recurrence/tail distribution, but it is not a universal prevalence prior.

## 5. Biological mechanics

### 5.1 Lineage and shared programs

Generate broad lineage identity plus multiple overlapping continuous programs that affect many genes with positive and negative loadings.

Requirements:
- donor recurrence;
- nontrivial covariance;
- overlapping gene support;
- broad/common and tail realizations;
- no single challenge factor dominates total molecular variance;
- enough intrinsic dimension to detect representation narrowing/collapse.

### 5.2 Regulatory/mechanistic programs

Extend the Stage81A3 synthetic-network precedent with multiple mechanistic program families.

At least:
- one RNA-visible regulatory mechanism;
- one RNA-partially-identifiable mechanism;
- one privileged-modality-dominant mechanism;
- one STAT1-like microglial specialist program informed by PR #311 calibration;
- additive and non-additive program interactions.

Synthetic causal edges are controller truth only. Stage75/SCENIC topology may inspire stress structure but does not become a human causal claim.

### 5.3 Query semantics

A query chooses an address/program question from the same biological system.

The generator does not create a separate query factor.

For each query case the controller defines:
- the biological quantity being queried;
- what teacher-side measured evidence can construct about that quantity;
- what student-side evidence is lawful;
- whether the increment beyond query scalar/identity is real, stochastic or absent.

Required controls:
- q-count-only;
- q-address-only;
- wrong-q;
- query-agnostic;
- teacher query-value intervention;
- target construction that cannot reduce to a disguised queried scalar.

### 5.4 Relational geometry

Relational truth is induced by the biological manifold.

The population must contain:
- stable broad/global ordering;
- mesoscale ordering;
- deliberately weak/non-qualified fine-locality structure so a correct pipeline does not promote hard nearest-local objectives merely because they are available.

Exact ties remain unresolved.

### 5.5 Generic interaction families for C6/C4 increment tests

Do not plant candidate-named `C6-positive` or `C4-positive` factors.

Instead create prospectively frozen mechanism families within the same population:
- purely additive shared + local/query mechanisms;
- shared×local non-additive mechanisms;
- purely additive shared + local + relational mechanisms;
- higher-order structured interactions involving relational/context structure.

Controller mapping determines which candidate should have a true increment. The fit side sees only observations and lawful query/context descriptors.

### 5.6 Context and perturbation

Context-responsive and context-insensitive programs coexist in the same population.

Perturbations act on selected mechanisms with matched null arms.

Requirements:
- true/shuffled/distant context controls;
- intrinsic-null context programs;
- no focal/adjacent shuffled-neighbor leakage;
- actual and matched-null interventions;
- common and rare response regimes;
- synthetic causal truth only.

## 6. From biological truth to expected molecular state

The generator produces a controller-visible, noise-free expected molecular state analogous to historical `lambda_norm`/expected-state semantics.

This state is the bridge between biology and observation.

The fit side does not receive latent factors or noise-free expected state unless a specific experiment prospectively defines such a teacher view; normal teacher/student qualification receives measured observations only.

## 7. Production address and input contract

### 7.1 Two scales

**Unit/mechanics fixtures** may use small dimensions for deterministic tests.

**Production qualification realization** must conform to the modern canonical reader/address contract so the actual downstream pipeline can ingest it without a synthetic-only model implementation.

Default production vocabulary:
- canonical 41,238-address namespace currently used by the project;
- measured/unmeasured support preserved explicitly;
- measured zero remains distinct from unmeasured;
- donor/source/operator/cell identity metadata follow the same semantic classes used by the reader.

If the runtime authority at implementation time names a different exact successor registry, the production synthetic contract must bind that exact registry prospectively rather than silently remap.

### 7.2 Cell counts

Cell counts per donor/source/lineage are derived prospectively from authenticated TRAIN distributions under a compute-bounded scaling rule.

The scaling rule must be frozen before qualification realization. Donor-primary estimands prevent compute packing from redefining scientific mass.

## 8. Acquisition and observation model

Biology is generated before source/operator/depth observation effects.

### 8.1 Acquisition selection versus biological causation

Source/operator cannot directly alter latent biological truth.

However, acquisition may sample biological states non-uniformly. This permits realistic source–biology association through selection/composition without treating source as a biological cause.

The simulator therefore separates:
1. biological population generation;
2. source/operator acquisition/sampling;
3. molecular observation/noise.

### 8.2 RNA observation

Use a two-part observation structure consistent with V79 lessons:
- structural measurement support;
- detection probability;
- positive-value distribution;
- depth and quadratic-depth dependence;
- source/acquisition observability;
- donor-associated observation structure where justified;
- optional operator perturbations as measurement stress;
- repeated observations where needed to estimate measurement uncertainty.

Q00–Q11 remain the dedicated qualification of this observation-model family; the unified simulator does not replace that campaign.

### 8.3 Same-nucleus RNA+ATAC

Both modalities arise from the same latent cell/nucleus.

Privileged accessibility/regulatory observations include a mixture of:
- redundant information;
- RNA-predictable but stochastic increment;
- teacher-private increment.

### 8.4 Separate-nucleus RNA/ATAC

RNA and ATAC cells are independently sampled from the same donor/lineage/program distribution.

No synthetic cell pairing is exposed. Evaluation is donor or donor×state level only.

### 8.5 Spatial/context observation

Coordinates/neighborhoods are generated as part of the same population/acquisition process. Context-conditioned and intrinsic-null mechanisms coexist.

### 8.6 Technical pseudo-rarity

Pseudo-rare cells coexist with true rare biology in the same observed population.

Their unusual observations are created by support/depth/noise/acquisition conditions while the relevant biological rare-program activity is absent.

## 9. Trust domains and blinding

### 9.1 Controller-only truth

Never available to fit-side code:
- latent factors/mechanisms;
- noise-free expected molecular state unless explicitly authorized as a controller diagnostic;
- program identity and activity labels;
- rare-tail labels;
- pseudo-rare labels;
- interaction/additivity class;
- counterfactual truth;
- recoverability class;
- expected candidate/gate terminal;
- oracle conditional uncertainty quantities.

### 9.2 Fit-side lawful data

Available only as required by the experiment:
- measured RNA;
- measured ATAC for privileged teacher experiments;
- physical context when permitted;
- query identity when permitted;
- support masks;
- donor split;
- lawful observation descriptors;
- intervention identity only for perturbation experiments.

### 9.3 Score side

After model outputs are frozen, controller scoring may join fit outputs to hidden truth and compute qualification results.

### 9.4 Realization split

Use separate prospectively frozen realizations for:
- development/calibration of generator mechanics and gate implementation;
- final qualification.

Final qualification truth remains sealed from model/gate development until all relevant rules are frozen. Selective reruns or seed replacement are forbidden.

## 10. Same downstream code path requirement

Synthetic success is useful only if it qualifies the pipeline intended for real data.

Therefore:

1. Reuse PR #313 tournament/case-ledger/field-firewall/falsifier/controller code directly wherever input contracts permit.
2. Candidate gate executors used on synthetic qualification must be the same callable executor implementations intended for real qualification; only dataset/truth adapters differ.
3. Reuse canonical teacher/student/EMA runtime for model qualification; do not create a synthetic-only learner that bypasses production mechanics.
4. Hash-bind shared executor/runtime code in qualification receipts.
5. Any necessary synthetic adapter must be thin, auditable and unable to access controller truth.

A synthetic-only parallel implementation does not qualify the real pipeline.

## 11. Teacher, oracle and student are different objects

### 11.1 Controller oracle

The controller knows biological truth. The controller oracle is **never** the teacher.

### 11.2 Genuine teacher construction

A teacher must construct its target/state from the evidence assigned to it.

Examples:
- rich lawful RNA;
- query-conditioned same-cell measured evidence;
- same-nucleus RNA+ATAC for privileged regulatory teacher experiments;
- physical context for context teacher experiments.

The teacher may not receive latent synthetic factors or controller labels.

Synthetic truth scores whether the teacher preserved/constructed the intended biology.

### 11.3 EMA

EMA stabilizes learned shared teacher/student modules after valid optimizer updates. It is not biological truth.

Privileged teacher-only adapters must have an explicit lawful construction/learning rule; they cannot simply be populated from synthetic oracle truth.

### 11.4 Student

The default student is one full-capacity partial-RNA backbone with block/functional heads when justified.

The student receives only lawful partial evidence and observation descriptors. Separate full students must be earned by stable negative transfer, incompatible lawful evidence or materially incompatible support/abstention geometry.

## 12. Recoverability and uncertainty

Recoverability is a property of the joint distribution of hidden biology and student evidence, not a label handed to the model.

Controller-side classes remain:
- deterministic/highly recoverable;
- predictable but stochastic;
- teacher-private;
- NOT_ESTIMABLE.

### 12.1 Biological uncertainty

Do not plant arbitrary `U_bio` coordinates.

Use the generative model to estimate the conditional variability/entropy of relevant hidden biological quantities given lawful student evidence.

### 12.2 Measurement uncertainty

Estimate from repeated observation draws / count splitting / depth perturbation under fixed latent biology.

### 12.3 Scoring

Student uncertainty and abstention are calibrated against controller-side conditional error/support, while keeping biological uncertainty and measurement uncertainty separate.

## 13. Synthetic rehearsal of the real TD candidate system

The same underlying population supports all candidates.

### C1_SHARED_EMERGENT

Test whether a candidate recovers shared/global biological structure, donor recurrence and transfer while resisting acquisition/depth/support shortcuts and preserving common and rare realizations.

### C2_RELATIONAL_SHARED

Test whether the candidate adds truthful broad/mesoscale relational information induced by the same biological manifold. Hard fine-locality should not be rewarded when the planted manifold does not contain it.

### C3_QUERY_MEASURED

Test true query-conditioned increment beyond q scalar/address, wrong-q, query-agnostic and technical controls. Teacher target construction must be audited so query value is not disguised as the target.

### C6_SHARED_QUERY_COMPOSITE

Test only if C1 and C3 analogues qualify. A fused representation earns increment only for mechanism families containing true non-additive shared×local information and must not be promoted on matched additive mechanism families.

### C4_STRUCTURED_COMPOSITE

Test only if relational and query constituents qualify. Structured fusion must preserve every constituent and earn increment only where higher-order structured interactions exist.

### C5_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST

Test regulatory/mechanistic information using lawful RNA and privileged modalities. C5 cannot rescue failure of all foundation biology and cannot force point prediction of teacher-private privileged realization.

## 14. Gate-executor qualification scope

The synthetic substrate should help qualify **decision machinery**, not manufacture human biological thresholds.

Synthetic qualification may establish:
- correct estimand implementation;
- expected direction/sign behavior;
- null/positive distinction;
- NOT_ESTIMABLE behavior;
- dependency logic;
- leakage/shortcut falsifiers;
- invariance and support handling;
- composite non-rescue semantics;
- software correctness.

Real-data numerical effect-size thresholds remain separately governed and require prospective outcome-blind real calibration/error-control justification where needed.

Executor/estimand/rule code is hash-bound before final synthetic qualification.

## 15. Portfolio convergence rehearsal

Run the same outcomes as real TD:
- single block;
- complementary multi-block portfolio;
- accountable fused implementation;
- specialist-augmented portfolio;
- no qualified portfolio.

Joint tests:
- complementarity/redundancy;
- destructive interference;
- common/rare preservation;
- held-donor transfer;
- support and abstention calibration;
- no aggregate rescue of a failed block.

## 16. Rich-teacher / partial-RNA rehearsal

After synthetic target-block qualification, run the same downstream decomposition intended for real data:

1. reconcile frozen RNA-derived biological quantities with privileged same-nucleus measurements;
2. classify privileged increments as recoverable, stochastic, teacher-private or NOT_ESTIMABLE;
3. test student point/distributional/abstention behavior accordingly;
4. test shared backbone plus heads against full-student challengers;
5. preserve block-level accountability.

Privileged evidence may characterize/falsify an interpretation but cannot retrospectively redefine the frozen RNA target object.

## 17. Learned-teacher continuity

After the target/teacher topology is frozen, run bounded qualification-only learning using the canonical runtime.

For each selected block/function, ask whether the learned teacher still preserves the controller-known biology:
- shared-state continuity;
- query-conditioned continuity;
- relational-order continuity;
- regulatory common/rare continuity;
- support/uncertainty separation;
- anti-collapse/non-narrowing.

Relational continuity is the synthetic analogue of TD60. Other blocks receive their corresponding block-specific continuity tests.

Failure classes:
- **PRESERVATION FAILURE** — teacher loses known biology;
- **INFERENCE LIMITATION** — teacher preserves it but student evidence cannot recover it;
- **UNCERTAINTY FAILURE** — insufficient/stochastic evidence is not reflected in uncertainty/abstention;
- **SHORTCUT FAILURE** — prediction is carried by acquisition/depth/query leakage instead of biology;
- **INTERFERENCE FAILURE** — joint architecture destroys a block that qualifies separately.

## 18. Transport rehearsal

Prospectively test:
1. held synthetic validation/oracle donor;
2. held acquisition/operator regime;
3. held source-like sampling regime;
4. held measurement-depth/support regime;
5. held modality/technology simulation where lawful.

Report biological novelty/support separately from measurement-domain OOD.

## 19. Development sequence

No stage below authorizes the next automatically.

1. **Historical integration receipt** — bind Stage81A3 biological simulator lineage, Q00–Q11 authorities and PR #311 reusable components.
2. **Truth schema** — candidate-agnostic biology and controller-only derived truth objects.
3. **Small deterministic generator fixtures** — latent state, expected molecular state, counterfactuals and lineage eligibility.
4. **Acquisition/observation adapters** — RNA first; then same-nucleus ATAC, separate-nucleus ATAC, context and perturbation.
5. **Trust-domain/firewall tests** — prove fit side cannot access controller truth.
6. **Modern reader-schema adapter** — production 41,238-address support and metadata contract.
7. **Development realization** — generator/mechanics and gate-implementation calibration only.
8. **Shared PR #313 executor adapter** — no duplicate synthetic tournament.
9. **Gate-executor semantic qualification** — positive/null/shortcut/NE fixtures.
10. **Final qualification contract and sealed realization** — seeds, populations, support and gates frozen before realization.
11. **Synthetic TD tournament and portfolio convergence**.
12. **Rich-teacher / partial-RNA recoverability experiment**.
13. **Shared-backbone interference comparison**.
14. **Bounded learned-EMA teacher continuity**.
15. **Transport rehearsal**.

## 20. Readiness criteria

The synthetic program is ready for final model/pipeline qualification only when all are true:

- historical Stage81A3 biological mechanics are explicitly assimilated rather than silently replaced;
- Q00–Q11 remain byte-immutable and separately auditable;
- one multi-lineage population contains common, intermediate and rare activity in the same program system;
- specialist programs obey biological eligibility/support rather than appearing in every cell;
- candidate names are absent from latent generator state;
- candidate truth is derived controller-side from the same hidden biological system;
- source/operator/depth do not directly create biology, while acquisition selection can create realistic associations;
- technical pseudo-rarity coexists with true rarity;
- full production output conforms to the modern reader/address/support contract;
- controller truth is physically firewalled from model fitting;
- the same downstream executor/runtime code intended for real data is exercised;
- the teacher constructs states from lawful measured evidence and is not replaced by the synthetic oracle;
- query target construction is q-safe and falsified against scalar/address/wrong-q controls;
- biological and measurement uncertainty are defined from the generative problem, not arbitrary labels;
- same-nucleus and separate-nucleus multimodal semantics are correct;
- additive and non-additive mechanisms permit honest C6/C4 positive and null increment tests without candidate-shaped planting;
- block failures cannot be rescued by aggregate composite score;
- common/rare preservation, interference, recoverability, continuity and transport can all be adjudicated;
- no pathology/protected real outcome is required;
- final qualification realization is prospective, sealed and non-replaceable.

## 21. Explicit non-claims

This design does not claim:
- synthetic mechanisms are validated human disease mechanisms;
- STAT1 is the final project target;
- Stage75/SCENIC is a causal human GRN;
- synthetic ATAC/context/perturbation amplitudes are universal human effects;
- synthetic effect-size thresholds are automatically real-data thresholds;
- the original PR #311 separate-world PASS qualifies this unified substrate;
- any TD target portfolio, representation or architecture has already qualified;
- any model fitting, scoring or training is authorized.
