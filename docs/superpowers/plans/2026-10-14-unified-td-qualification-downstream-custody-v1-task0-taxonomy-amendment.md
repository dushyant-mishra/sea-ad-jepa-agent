## `HISTORICAL_BIOLOGICAL_EVIDENCE`

`HISTORICAL_BIOLOGICAL_EVIDENCE` means **frozen historical biological evidence bound to the exact execution law under which it was produced**.

Objects in this class:

- retain their original scientific result and provenance as pre-merger evidence;
- do not inherit current unified producer/executor semantics;
- do not confer current TD, registry, target, representation, Stage4, or training authority;
- cannot be used to promote a current gate or candidate merely because the historical result was favorable;
- cannot be rerun merely for migration/custody classification;
- require a new prospective execution identity and the current unified qualification chain before any current-authority use.

PR #314's one spent corrected TD replay is the motivating instance. Its Pattern-A result remains historical biological evidence under the exact old corrected-replay execution law and is not silently reinterpreted as evidence under the unified architecture.
