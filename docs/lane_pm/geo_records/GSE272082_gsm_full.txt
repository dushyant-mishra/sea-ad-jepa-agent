The homogenized tissue was filtered through a 35 μm cell strainer. Small cell debris were removed by centrifuging at 300g for 3 minutes at 4 °C. The large cell debris was removed by gradient centrifugation using OptiPrep density gradient medium (Sigma, cat#D1556-250ML) at 7200g for 15 minutes at 4 °C.
