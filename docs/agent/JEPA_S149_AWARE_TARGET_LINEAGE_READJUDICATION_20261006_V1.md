## Why this re-adjudication exists

The target program has already undergone extensive discovery and falsification. S149 does **not** authorize restarting target discovery from scratch. Instead, S149 changes the interpretation of one important evidence class: pooled real gene-gene detection/topology statistics can be strongly driven by study/cohort measurement composition.

The task here is therefore to distinguish historical candidates that were genuinely biologically falsified from candidates that were only:

- mechanically invalid;
- shortcut-dominated;
- q-leaky;
- non-identifiable in a synthetic fixture;
- unqualified because the real evidence source was confounded by measurement/study composition.

Only the last four classes may remain scientifically live, and even then only under prospective S149-aware re-testing.

## First scientific conclusion

The historical record does **not** justify a fresh target brainstorm yet.

The scientifically live set is narrower:

1. TD41–TD43-style **cell-specific pair-order / excess-over-null structure**;
2. **value-blind query-local target constructions** from the PR #163 family;
3. **program-level targets** that can be defined without pooled cohort covariance and reproduced independently inside observation processes;
4. **structured combined targets** only if they add transportable biology beyond global/operator/source information.

The following stay closed and should not be rerun as if untested:

- identity-dominated T0/T1/TCTX-style targets;
- broad static/context sufficiency arguments;
- donor-centering / V6R5B residual rescue;
- historical T1/u205 learned states or checkpoints;
- synthetic T_A/T_B choice as target authority.

## S149-aware retest principle

A historical target candidate can survive only if its evidence passes three distinct questions:

### A. Within-observation-process existence

Does the target structure exist independently within HVS, NPH52 and SEA-AD (or another physically justified operator grouping), using donor-aware estimation and gene/feature construction fit inside that stratum rather than inherited from pooled covariance?

### B. Cross-observation-process transport

Does the target preserve the same biologically relevant relationship across observation processes after a lawful TRAIN-only alignment, fitted without held-out donors influencing the alignment?

The target need not have identical raw coordinates across studies. Stable subspace may be sufficient, but coordinate semantics may only be claimed if coordinate stability is actually demonstrated.

### C. Shortcut increment

Does the candidate add material information beyond:

- gene/address identity;
- global cell summaries;
- donor/source/study/operator;
- depth/support/missingness;
- query-value and q-derived preprocessing leakage;
- matched wrong-cell / population-prior controls appropriate to the candidate.

A high pooled prediction score cannot answer this question.

## Estimand rule introduced by S149

Every re-test must declare the population quantity being estimated before outcomes are examined.

At minimum, candidate analyses must distinguish:

- cell-weighted empirical estimand;
- donor-weighted estimand;
- source-balanced donor-weighted estimand;
- any hierarchical/tempered alternative.

No candidate may be called failed or successful because it looks better under a post-hoc change of estimand.
