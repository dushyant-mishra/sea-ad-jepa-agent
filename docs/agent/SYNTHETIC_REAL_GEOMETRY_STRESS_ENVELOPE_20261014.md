# Synthetic real-geometry stress envelope

Date: 2026-10-14
Status: `EMPIRICAL_STRESS_ENVELOPE_ONLY__NO_HUMAN_BIOLOGICAL_AUTHORITY__NO_IMPLEMENTATION_AUTHORITY`

Purpose: preserve the empirically observed production geometry that synthetic known-truth worlds must stress, without turning observed acquisition/population statistics into latent human biology or mandatory literal synthetic frequencies.

## Authoritative descriptive anchors

Reader-fit population profile currently records:
- 4,553,407 reader-fit cells;
- 104 reader-fit donors;
- 42 operators;
- 1,400 donor×operator groups;
- 41,238 canonical molecular addresses;
- donor cell-count range: **81 to 174,111**;
- operator cell-count range: **179 to 732,360**;
- donor×operator group-size range: **1 to 42,209** cells;
- donor operator-count range: **1 to 24** operators per donor;
- global cell-weighted measured fraction of the 41,238-address universe: approximately **0.8310**.

The real geometry is therefore strongly ragged and imbalanced. Equal donor sizes, equal operator sizes, equal donor×operator occupancy, and globally uniform support are not faithful descriptions of the reader-fit substrate.

A separate support-overlap audit records **17,186 / 41,238 = 41.675%** of canonical addresses as measured scalar in all 42 operators, establishing substantial incomplete-operator support across the address universe.

## What production synthetic qualification must contain

At minimum, include prospectively frozen worlds/challenge strata with:

1. balanced donor/operator geometry as a control;
2. donor-count imbalance spanning orders of magnitude;
3. operator-count imbalance spanning orders of magnitude;
4. ragged donor×operator occupancy including very small and very large groups;
5. source-asymmetric support masks;
6. addresses supported across many operators and addresses supported across few or zero required operators;
7. held-out-donor conditions;
8. held-out operator/source acquisition conditions;
9. sparse-support conditions where an estimand remains identifiable;
10. support/rank conditions that are deliberately `NOT_ESTIMABLE_BY_CONSTRUCTION`.

A balanced toy world alone is insufficient.

## Empirical-envelope versus synthetic-stress distinction

Do not force every world to reproduce the literal extrema above. Use two classes:

### Empirical-envelope worlds
Observation/population parameters are constrained by authenticated real TRAIN/reader-fit descriptive distributions. These worlds test whether downstream mechanics survive realistic acquisition/population heterogeneity.

### Prospective stress worlds
Parameters may extend beyond observed ranges to probe failure boundaries. They must be labeled synthetic stress and cannot be described as observed human prevalence, effect size, or frequency.

## Source-asymmetric support rule

The support architecture must preserve source/operator asymmetry. Exact source-level measured-address fractions must be re-derived and hash-bound from the current authoritative support artifact before implementation freezes numeric calibration. Do not copy an approximate percentage from prose when an exact support table is available.

This requirement exists because the scientific point is structural asymmetry, not a particular rounded percentage. Numeric source fractions are observation-calibration inputs only; they do not set latent biological prevalence.

## Sampling and aggregation consequences

Synthetic downstream stress tests must be able to detect methods that accidentally weight cells instead of donors where donor is the primary inferential unit.

Include adversarial cases where:
- one/few donors dominate the cell count;
- one/few operators dominate the cell count;
- a biological effect is consistent donor-wise but swamped by cell weighting;
- a nuisance/source effect looks strong cell-wise but is inconsistent donor-wise;
- support availability differs with source/operator frequency.

Qualification should fail mechanisms that obtain apparent evidence merely from the largest donors/operators when the governing gate is donor-primary.

## Support-state law

Preserve the three-state address observation semantics plus measured zero:
- `STRUCTURALLY_UNMEASURED`;
- `MEASURED_SCALAR`;
- `MEASURED_COLLISION_UNRESOLVED`;
- measured scalar zero is a valid measurement, not missingness.

Sparse support must not be repaired by imputation or zero coercion.

These empirical geometry statistics may constrain:
- sampling/allocation;
- observation support;

- depth/capture distributions;
- calibration and stress-world composition.

They may not silently define:
- latent state prevalence;
- causal effect sizes;
- disease biology;
- target winners;
- representation winners;
