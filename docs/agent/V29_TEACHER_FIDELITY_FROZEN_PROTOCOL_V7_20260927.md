# Frozen protocol v7 — the sham must be real genes, and a stopping rule

**Prospectively dated successor to v6. v1–v6 preserved unedited. v7 is the sole
execution authority.**

**Dated 2026-09-27. Gate v2 FAILED — NEG-2 false-qualified in 39 of 40 datasets.
No control gene has been extracted and no real expression has been read.**

## The measured reason v6 failed, and why it could not have worked

Partial correlation with log capture efficiency, **controlling for log D**:

| | partial corr(amplitude, log cap \| log D) |
|---|---|
| real program | **+0.4437** |
| sham, abundance/sparsity/capture-matched | +0.0031 |
| sham, reliability-matched | +0.0092 |

Both shams were **constructed from the observed denominator `D`**. Their only
route to capture efficiency is `D` itself, so once `log D` is a control they
retain no capture information at all. The real program is driven by the
**latent** capture factor directly, and `D` is merely a noisy function of it, so
the real program keeps capture information that `log D` does not absorb.

**Any sham synthesised from observable quantities must systematically
under-carry the nuisance.** No amount of marginal matching repairs that: it is a
structural property of how the sham is generated, not of which moments it
matches.

## The fix, verified

A sham generated by the **same mechanism** as the real program — driven by the
latent capture factor, with an independent latent — is exchangeable:

| | partial(amp, readout \| log D) | partial(amp, log cap \| log D) |
|---|---|---|
| real | +0.1970 | +0.4410 |
| same-mechanism sham | +0.1939 | +0.4520 |

**Consequence for real data, and it simplifies the implementation:** the sham
cannot be synthesised. It must be **real gene quadruples drawn from the same
nuclei**, because real genes are driven by the same latent capture factor the
program is. They are matched on abundance, sparsity and within-quadruple
coherence, and must be disjoint from every program gene, every reserved readout
and every control gene.

A real-gene sham may occasionally carry genuine biology related to `Q`. That
makes the null **conservative**, which is the right direction for a gate.

## Stopping rule, pre-committed

This is the **last attempt to qualify the count-based readout**.

> If NEG-1 or NEG-2 remains systematically positive — a false-qualification rate
> materially above nominal across the calibration datasets — the count-based
> readout is recorded as **INSUFFICIENTLY_IDENTIFIABLE under the tested nuisance
> conditions**, and work moves to the composition experiment. Thresholds,
> matching rules and control definitions are **not** adjusted again to make it
> pass.

That failure would be a statement about the **validation strategy**, not
evidence that the per-nucleus biological state is absent. Recording it as the
latter would be a false negative dressed as a finding.

## Regimes the gate must survive

Including regimes **not used to tune the sham**:

| regime | capture sd | partner mean |
|---|---|---|
| A (tuning) | 0.6 | 1.2 sparse |
| B (tuning) | 0.6 | 40 dense |
| C (untuned) | 1.0 | 1.2 sparse |
| D (untuned) | 0.3 | 0.6 very sparse |

Both negative arms must fail qualification and both positive arms must qualify,
in **every** regime, with calibration across independent datasets per regime.

## The alternative is not assumed clean

If work moves to the composition readout, `Q`'s composition is **not**
automatically nuisance-free. At one to four molecules, pseudocounts and dropout
make a centred log-ratio sensitive to capture efficiency. Its measurement
reliability must be established first, and a count-based compositional model
conditional on `Q`'s total is the form to consider. The fallback inherits none
of the scrutiny already applied unless it is given it deliberately.

## Everything else

Unchanged from v6 and v5: NB GLM on raw nonnegative counts with the frozen
`log D` offset; predictors leave-one-out centered within `donor × operator`
stratum, readout never centered; sham-generated null with
`p = (1 + count of sham at or above real) / (1 + B)`; permutation retained as a
diagnostic only; the two-thirds donor criterion; BH at q = 0.05 across the six
directed tests; minimum stratum 20; held-out donors by hash; frozen denominator
with structural-missingness handling; reserved genes frozen; `TRAINING=OFF`.

**The biological objective is unchanged:** does `P`'s four-partner state predict
something about `Q` beyond the queried gene, the technical nuisance, and an
equally reliable sham?
