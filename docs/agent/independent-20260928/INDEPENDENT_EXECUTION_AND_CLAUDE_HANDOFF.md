## Completed: exact disjoint sham feasibility tool

`exact_sham_panel_feasibility.py` implements the **exact** Hall/bipartite-matching upper bound for four predeclared slot pools. For nonoverlapping K quadruples, the maximum integer K equals the minimum across all 15 nonempty slot subsets S of `floor(|union(pool[i], i in S)| / |S|)`. `witness_allocation` constructs a disjoint roster using a sparse matching operation and refuses excessive graphs rather than guessing. 120 random small universes checked against an independent exhaustive DFS, plus explicit 199-panel proof/witness and overlapping/empty cases.
