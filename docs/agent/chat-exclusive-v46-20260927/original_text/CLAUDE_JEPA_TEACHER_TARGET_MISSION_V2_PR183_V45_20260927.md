## The single scientific problem

Find a target that a **teacher actually constructs from the richer biological measurements assigned to it**, that reflects a **query-associated multidimensional cellular state**, and that a student can recover from **strictly less, lawful complementary RNA**. An embedding is not biological because it is latent; success at reconstructing a separate RNA panel is not proof of teacher fidelity. A q-visible teacher is allowed as a scientific candidate, not selected by default: it must add independently testable biological utility beyond a q-count-only predictor. A q-blind teacher is a matched comparator, not automatically preferable. Preserve support, missingness, approximate sampling uncertainty, donor variability and potential rare biology.

**Deliver an actual target-decision experiment, not another generic architecture tournament or a handoff that silently converts design into validation.** PR #183's scientific corrections and the exact V45 local transfer are already available; do not spend the assignment reproducing handoffs or reacquiring completed ETL.
