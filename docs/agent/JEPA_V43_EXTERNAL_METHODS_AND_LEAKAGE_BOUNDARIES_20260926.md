## Exact original sources and narrow transferable claims

| Source | Primary original publication | What V43 could borrow | What it does NOT justify |
|---|---|---|---|
| Theia, Shang et al. | [PMLR CoRL 2025](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v270/shang25a.html) | Heterogeneous teacher features distilled through teacher-specific adapters to a compact student; **leave-one-teacher-out**, equivalent-capacity single teacher and independent feature information comparisons | Vision feature diversity is not biological fidelity; adding experts is not proof of independent molecular signals. |
| M3-JEPA, Lei et al. | [PMLR ICML 2025](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/lei25b.html) | Predictive latent alignment with multi-gate experts to distinguish common from modality-specific structure. Research arm B tests shared trunk/multiple heads against arm C's genuinely separate teachers. | Its image/text/audio cross-modal gates are not an excuse to feed donor, study, support mask or **unknown test outcomes** to a biological selection gate. |
| MultiVI, Ashuach et al. | [Nature Methods 2023](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41592-023-01909-9) | Modality-specific encoders, observed/missing modality handling, probabilistic uncertainty and explicit support bookkeeping | Imputed ATAC is not an observed fragment and never becomes an independent test label. Single-cell paired claims cannot be borrowed for separate-nucleus GSE174367. |
| scGLUE, Cao and Gao | [Nature Biotechnology 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-022-01284-4) | Distinct modality encoders aligned through a prior guidance graph even on **unpaired** RNA/ATAC. A regulatory teacher can be formulated as a later separately tested arm. | Genomic proximity/motif priors are **hypotheses**, not causal evidence or reliable held-out truth. Our historical Stage71 random-graph control was more predictive than the tested real graph; never assert graph utility a priori. |
| Cell-JEPA, ElSheikh et al. | [arXiv 2602.02093 (2026-02-02)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.02093) | Partial-view student and EMA teacher targeting stable cell embeddings; compare against a **single teacher** and pure count-reconstruction controls. The paper combines latent JEPA with a gene-reconstruction objective. | Better zero-shot cell-type clustering does not prove query-specific state or intervention-effect accuracy; paper reports no analogous effect-size gain for its perturbation study. Our target must remain non-scalar, with any count head diagnostic and quarantined. |
| CoMAD, Mandalika et al. | [arXiv 2508.04816 (2025-08-06)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.04816) | Teacher-specific linear adapters and normalization; asymmetric evidence (student sparser than rich teachers), token-level vs global representation tests | **Consensus gating is unsafe for rare biology:** a minority specialist can be correct when dominant common teacher disagrees. Do not gate it out via cosine consensus. Preprint and vision-domain only. |

Theia and CoMAD exemplify complementary off-the-shelf teachers, unlike our EMA teachers derived from a student. V43 must specify **teacher source, optimizer/EMA parent, initialization, independence of training data or objective, and frozen checkpoint lineage** for each candidate. Multiple EMA copies of one student plus cloned inputs are *not* independent biological teachers. If teacher heads share a trunk, classify as arm B, not arm C. Cross-teacher student-space alignment must use training-only lawful data and can introduce shortcut leakage; score alignment on donor-heldout q-specific controls before reliance.

## What is immediately implementable vs experimental

1. **Now, CPU and no real training:** audited `TeacherComponent` tuple `(head_id, same_cell_key, canonical_query, embedding, observed_support, biological_eligibility, provenance, uncertainty)`. Do not fuse data from different same-cell IDs into a claimed same-cell target. A modality from separate nuclei can contribute *population/donor-level supervision only*, not an absent per-cell field. Output a structured **shared + fine + optional rare** target retaining each head; missing evidence gives `None` + explicit absence, not zeros. A support-routing algorithm may read **only prospective measured/missing support and fixed eligibility**, never agreement, observed target scalar, donor/study technical identity, protected outcome or post-hoc model accuracy.
2. **Next, source/semantics:** per-head biological falsifier and query-count policy. A rich teacher **may** use true q in the biological target, but quantify scalar decodability and residual biological information. Student's ostensibly missing q must be invariant to a q-only count counterfactual all the way from raw preprocessing, pooled/global features and library denominator through model inputs. Any q-excluded teacher additionally requires q-counterfactual invariance and readout proof.
3. **Then, teacher-only development data (requires prospective lawful selection):** independent gene-view stability, donor-heldout biological program reproducibility, native-support stratification, teacher-removal ablation, capacity/compute-matched single rich teacher and shared trunk, same q vs wrong q, random/shuffled expert, rare-donor recurrence and uncertainty calibration. Retain no-confidence predictions as abstentions, not evidence of better biology.
4. **Finally, only with scientific authorization:** bounded matched student research; compare held-out prediction of **independently measured** biology, within-support q-local information and rare recurrence, not just JEPA loss or cross-teacher cosine. Maintain source/measurement stratification and a fully specified honest test multiplicity rule.

## Preserved external evaluation wall

Lane D [#164](https://github.com/dushyant-mishra/sea-ad-jepa-agent/pull/164) reported GSE174367 snRNA and snATAC in **separate nuclei** sharing 18 sample/donor groups, with 2 extra ATAC-only samples. Its unused ATAC count matrix is an option for an **independent** modality-level test. No GSE174367 ATAC counts, peak labels derived from them, test-set model-selection results or their descendants may be used to train, gate, tune, select or freeze the very teacher being scored on this ATAC outcome. If a **multimodal teacher** uses that ATAC set for development, those observations must be excluded as an independent test, and an entirely disjoint approved ATAC evaluation resource must be obtained. The original Stage75F RNA-derived edges are **not** independent truth; the historic shuffled graph issue demands structural-distinctness controls.

No blanket statement that matching RNA and ATAC donors implies the *same individual cell*; donor-level association and cell-level paired inference have different estimands. No copied donor identifiers from an external collection may establish biological disjointness of people across repositories when the namespaces do not permit it.

## Four immediate failure modes to plant in code

- A missing fine/rare channel becomes a 0-valued *measured* tensor: **reject**, not silence.
- A rare head predicts real minority state, while two core heads disagree: **retain rare state + uncertainty** when independently eligible; don't use expert majority vote.
- A teacher from a different nucleus/assay is accidentally joined on donor ID and labeled `same_cell`: **reject**. Population-level cross-modal objectives must have a separately approved, explicit correspondence level.
- All specialist teachers are perfectly copied versions of the shared teacher: the system may pass shape and scalar consistency, but **must fail** its novel-biology/teacher-removal diagnostic.
