## Executive decision

1. **The present V5 teacher is not an approved scientific target.** `inactive_update_reference.py` sends an all-zero teacher hidden-target mask while measured expression still contains the queried value. This provides a direct informational route from the queried scalar to the teacher target. It is not evidence of a separately measured contextual state. PR #163 establishes the source path; PR #178 preserves its implications.
2. **The R8 four-partner measured target is a genuine same-cell RNA measurement and a usable *developmental anchor*, not validated biology or an approved neural latent target.** Its components are activity, support-aware composition, observation/missingness and count-sampling uncertainty. It is invariant to a raw q-only intervention by construction; this invariant does not establish target quality.
3. **The historical original-data studies have already tested predictability and preliminary coherence.** The APOE anchor has encouraging complementary-RNA predictability (heldout R² 0.355 vs technical 0.088 in the disjoint-reference diagnostic) but its prespecified separate RNA readouts were not successfully predicted in R7. P2RY12 and HLA-DRA did not consistently outperform technical baselines. No program has passed independent chromatin/protein fidelity. Do **not** call APOE a qualified teacher or infer an architecture winner.
4. **Normalization is an unclosed scientific estimand, not a trivial model patch.** Original-data R16 finds leakage from q *and* forbidden partner/reference genes through denominators and query-swap-only input differences; common 23-gene exclusion removes these specific shortcuts on 84 original historical cells, not across the whole FULL104 neural path. Claude's later source trace establishes that the audited masking/calibration reader and neural update harness are presently disjoint; no authenticated production neural reader rule has been established.
5. **Stop architecture expansion and new production training.** The actionable sequence is target measurement reliability on already available lawful original/public RNA; a *prospectively frozen* independent biological endpoint with demonstrated donor support and no outcome-based target selection; then a bounded actual teacher-only test against full permitted-RNA and technical comparators. Student distillation and teacher-team comparisons come **after** teacher fidelity, not before. If qualified independent data are absent, record `BIOLOGY_NOT_TESTABLE_AT_AVAILABLE_POWER`, not a software success.

### Hard stops and outcome interpretation

- Sparse or absent target/reference coverage => **abstention**, not fake zero or a smaller unreported selected subcohort.
- Student predicts same-assay anchor, but anchor has no added prespecified biology => **RNA predictability only**, not latent-state success.
- Frozen independent ATAC effect is null/underpowered => **no demonstrated independent biological fidelity** (do not claim biological absence or tune on the reserved outcome).
- Neural teacher adds nothing beyond full lawful RNA/technical controls => **teacher enrichment not demonstrated**; do not solve by increasing capacity or adding specialists.
- q-swap comparison changes normalized inputs, mask geometry, QC or target coordinates => **invalid q-specific biological comparison** until the change is removed or appropriately isolated.
- Any missing root, forged critical test evidence, unreadable provenance or authorization mismatch => **TRAINING OFF** independent of science outcome.
