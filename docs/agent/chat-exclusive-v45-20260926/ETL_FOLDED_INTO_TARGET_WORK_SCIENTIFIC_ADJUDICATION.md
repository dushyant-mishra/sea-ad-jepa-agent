## 1. Explicit correction to the earlier Morabito assessment

The prior broad claim that Morabito ATAC is unusable as an independent biological outcome merely because aggregate accessibility was inspected is **withdrawn**. “Accessed,” “used to build source annotation/ETL,” “used to select a target,” “used to train a teacher,” “used to tune a model,” and “used to evaluate a frozen model” are different events. The chronological record establishes the earlier candidate teacher designs and planned donor-level ATAC evaluation predated PR #175's aggregate accessibility summary. The later V43/V44 architecture work **postdates** PR #175; neither its chronology nor an absence of reported ATAC-based choices proves absolute blinding. Review source and decisions, not presumed motives. No committed evidence reviewed here shows an ATAC-driven architecture selection, and no donor-level ATAC benchmark has executed.

Morabito remains a **conditionally usable independent-modality, external-study donor-level benchmark**. Its snRNA and snATAC were collected from **different nuclei within the same brains**, with 18 shared microglial donor IDs in the completed Lane D comparison; never invent same-cell pairing or use cell counts as biological replicate n. Stage75F target edges derive from the cohort's RNA and cannot be independent RNA-to-ATAC truth. PR #175's *observed aggregate ATAC feature states* were inspected and are not a pristine selection oracle; the explicit condition is a reviewed endpoint/model/threshold lineage with no adaptation to those states. For any architecture changes after PR #175, document their actual inputs and rationale before interpreting the eventual ATAC test. PR #164 originally marked P6 cleared before PR #175; this change requires an updated exposure declaration, **not** a presumption that the model was contaminated or that the ATAC modality was trained on.

## 4. What this changes in our teacher-target adjudication

The hypothesis remains a **query-conditioned, multidimensional state constructed by a richer teacher and recoverable by a less-informed student**. It is not a commitment to q-blinding the teacher. A measured q-excluded four-partner anchor is an explicit developmental measurement; its invariance to q does not make a q-visible learned teacher unscientific, prove q-blind T_B better, or validate a latent state. T_A and T_B both require the same independently interpretable endpoint.

**Source-evidence layers must stay separate:** (1) existing FULL104/historical real RNA can establish whether a proposed anchor is measurable, donor-reproducible and predictive under lawful feature masking, but it remains same-assay biology; (2) Morabito's donor-level ATAC provides a possible independent *modality* test only under the qualified exposure and donor-join contract; (3) existing measured CRISPR perturbation effects are context and prospective **functional** challenge candidates only within each source's actual organism/cell-model, replication and exposure limits. The present recovered GSE178317 Day-8 screen does not perturb our three q genes and cannot be retroactively called direct q perturbation validation. The two-donor GSE301119 macrophage source overlaps q/partner gene identities but cannot stand in for independently replicated microglial mechanisms, especially while its statistical estimator is disqualified. GSE254205 is APOE cell-model drug-response development, not a patient cohort or therapy result.

**No target selection by external observed outcomes.** Keep original three hypothesis lists fixed for the next evidence audit, use only authenticated feature identity and source-provenance metadata to calculate feasibility, and explicitly label biological outcome values previously inspected as `DEVELOPMENT/INSPECTED`. If a source genuinely influenced an architecture choice, record the exact decision and restrict any resulting evaluation claim. Absence of a recorded influence is not proof that it never occurred; avoid both unsupported contamination allegations and unsupported pristine-holdout claims.

**No automatic qualification:** None of the ETL memberships, target-engagement numbers, R8 student R², Stage75F motif/coactivity evidence or PR175 gene coverage by itself qualifies a neural JEPA teacher. The decisive comparison remains teacher fidelity to a prespecified, appropriately independent measured endpoint, at the correct unit of replication, against technical, q-only, generic-cell and **complete lawful same-RNA** comparators. The actual V5 neural reader is not yet bound to the authenticated masking reader. Actual FULL104 training stays OFF; current scientific authority remains 0/33 fully closed.
