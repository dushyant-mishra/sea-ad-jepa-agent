## Implications for representation comparison

The target audit does not pre-select `cell_state`. Future qualification should compare at least:

1. global cell state;
2. gene/query-local state;
3. program-level state;
4. combined structured state.

A target may be useful for one level and invalid for another. RNA prediction alone does not promote the representation to transferable biology, regulatory support, or causal qualification.
