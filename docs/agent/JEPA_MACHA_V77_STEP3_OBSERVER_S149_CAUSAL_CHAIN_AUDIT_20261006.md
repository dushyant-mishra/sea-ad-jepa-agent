## What survives as simulator mechanics

Several controlled engineering findings remain useful:

- Poisson counting can strongly attenuate a planted synthetic correlation structure;
- changing abundance/capture coupling changes synthetic marginals and graph geometry;
- deterministic thresholding and Poisson realization can produce very different observed graphs from the same latent rates;
- stronger coherent rate changes make planted modules easier to see after counting noise.

These are properties of the simulator, not evidence that real microglia use the same latent mechanism.

## Spillover consequence

The observer architecture introduced during this sequence may still be useful as a measurement model, but its **selection rationale must not be inherited as biological proof**.

Any future promotion needs prospective validation against measurement-aware, within-observation-process real-data quantities rather than the historical pooled topology.
