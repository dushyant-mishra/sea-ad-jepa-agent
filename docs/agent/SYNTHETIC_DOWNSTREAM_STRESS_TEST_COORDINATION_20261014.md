## Required synthetic challenge families for downstream qualification

### Population and support
- balanced control world;
- highly imbalanced donor counts;
- highly imbalanced operator counts;
- ragged donor×operator occupancy;
- source-asymmetric measured-address support;
- sparse support and collision-unresolved support;
- held-out donors;
- held-out operators/source domains;
- zero-supported and rank-deficient cases that are controller `NOT_ESTIMABLE_BY_CONSTRUCTION`.

Balanced worlds are controls only and cannot be the sole qualification substrate.

### Biological complexity
- common programs;
- rare programs;
- weak/null mechanisms;
- positive and negative loadings;
- overlapping programs;
- additive and non-additive interactions;
- donor effects;
- context states;
- active perturbations and matched perturbation nulls;
- technical pseudo-rarity that must not activate true rare biology.

### Evidence versus measurement
Biological evidence challenge:
- one frozen already-generated observation bundle;
- strict nested evidence-right reveal;
- no acquisition regeneration between levels;
- terminal full rights;
- explicit physical added support;
- under-resolved rights ladder → NOT_ESTIMABLE.

Measurement-depth challenge:
- hidden biology and intended evidence class fixed;
- vary only count/depth/capture/repeated measurement realization;
- support changes induced by depth remain measurement-domain behavior;
- never relabel depth gain as biological-evidence gain.

### Biological novelty versus measurement OOD
Require all four joint classes when lawful:
1. familiar biology + familiar measurement;
2. familiar biology + held-out measurement;
3. novel biology + familiar measurement;
4. novel biology + held-out measurement.

Technology/source identity cannot directly define latent novelty.
