# Robust JEPA pipeline audit after JEPA-Anything

Date: 2026-10-13
Status: `NON_AUTHORIZING_ARCHITECTURE_AUDIT__NO_TARGET_OR_REPRESENTATION_SELECTION__TRAINING_OFF`

## 1. Objective

The objective of this audit is **not** to beat JEPA-Anything as a benchmark headline. The objective is to build the most scientifically robust JEPA pipeline justified by the project's months of target-discovery, representation, observation-operator, rare-program, relational, multimodal and teacher/student work.

JEPA-Anything (arXiv:2609.20800v1) is treated as useful external evidence that factorized predictive pathways can improve capacity allocation and state synthesis. It does not replace target discovery, biological semantics, donor/source transport, rare-state qualification, student recoverability, uncertainty separation or failure-scope governance.

This document changes no target authority, PR316 synthetic-test qualification, PR313 real-tournament authority, TD60, Stage4 or training status.

## 2. What JEPA-Anything changes for us

The paper makes one point we should take seriously: a single predictor acting on one monolithic target can allocate capacity poorly when predictable modes have different scale/difficulty. Dedicated predictive pathways can reduce gradient conflict and repeated directions.

However, OPF factorizes a **chosen latent target** by learned Euclidean subspaces. Under exact orthogonality with total factor width equal to the latent width, this is an information-preserving change of basis of the target state. Therefore OPF can improve optimization/capacity allocation, but it cannot make a biologically wrong target correct.

For this project, the central scientific problem remains upstream:

- what is the biological target object;
- what part is shared/global;
- what part is query-conditioned;
- what relational structure is real and estimable;
- what rare biology must be preserved;
- what privileged regulatory/context information is RNA-recoverable versus stochastic versus teacher-private;
- what variation is biological versus measurement/operator structure.

Those are not solved by orthogonality.

## 3. Historical project work that must not be reinvented

The project already established or prospectively froze most of the scientific structure that superficially looks like a response to OPF:

1. **Stable subspaces/blocks, not arbitrary axes.** If donor-balanced refits preserve a subspace while coordinates rotate, only the subspace is accountable. Coordinate semantics require separate evidence.
2. **Observation process separated from biology.** Technology/depth/support are modeled as the observation process, not unrestricted biological identities.
3. **Biological-evidence versus measurement-depth response curves.** More genes/evidence and better measurement of the same evidence are distinct uncertainty axes.
4. **Biological novelty versus measurement OOD.** Rare but well-measured biology must not be collapsed into technical outlier status.
5. **Target portfolio rather than forced single winner.** C1/C3/C2 and qualified specialists may coexist if they add non-redundant lawful information.
6. **Rich teacher / poorer student.** Privileged teacher information is partitioned into recoverable, stochastic/distributional and teacher-private components; private state is not a deterministic student point target.
7. **Rare biology is cross-cutting.** Rare/common preservation is required within active biological blocks; rarity is not a separate target family.
8. **Composite non-compensation.** C6/C4 cannot average away failure of their constituent biological claims.
9. **Relational claims have explicit failure scope.** Fine-scale failure cannot erase mesoscale or unrelated biology.
10. **No aggregate-score winner.** Leakage, shortcut, transport and support requirements are absolute gates.

These principles remain stronger scientific requirements than simply making latent factors mutually orthogonal.

## 5. Smoke results and what they do / do not mean

### 5.1 C1 shared/global state

Historical complementary-RNA ceilings on held-out donors are extremely high for common/distributed programs but materially weaker for innovation/rare regimes. Held-out-combined R2 is ~0.98-1.00 for several common/local programs, ~0.58 for the innovation tail, while rare-5% AP is ~0.34 and rare-1% AP ~0.03.

At the same time, the historical u205 representations retain very strong source/support information: source is perfectly classifiable from rich_H/partial_H in the shortcut atlas and support-measured-count is nearly perfectly recoverable.

Interpretation:

- a shared recoverable RNA state is plausible;
- broad predictive success cannot substitute for source/operator/depth/support falsifiers;
- rare preservation must remain constitutive;
- historical T1 representations are not production target authority merely because common-program R2 is high.

### 5.2 C2 relational state

A new non-authorizing smoke reconstructed the seven independent historical program directions from two deterministic disjoint halves of the repaired gene-address space.

Within donor:

- median disjoint-view pairwise-distance Spearman = **0.827**;
- lower quartile = **0.793**;
- wrong-cell median = **0.002**;
- median kNN10 overlap = **0.396** versus **0.125** wrong-cell;
- all 104 eligible donors had observed correlation and neighbor overlap above their smoke wrong-cell controls;
- source medians were HVS 0.832, NPH52 0.810, SEA_AD 0.834.

This supports the premise that distributed cross-view relational structure exists in the repaired pilot corpus. It does **not** establish C2, locality, scale, or the PR316 decision rule. The blinded synthetic qualification remains necessary.

### 5.3 C3 query-conditioned state

A deliberately crude held-donor proxy used eight query genes and query-specific eight-gene same-cell target panels while removing direct query and target-panel contributions from the molecular context scores.

Median held-donor R2:

- context proxy: **0.853**;
- q-count only: **0.712**;
- technical+q: **0.873**;
- context+correct-q: **0.854**;
- context+wrong-q: **0.872**.

The correct q scalar provided essentially no median increment over context (+0.001), while a wrong-q scalar sometimes looked better.

This is **not a C3 failure** because the proxy is not the prospectively frozen C3 target. It is an important stress result: generic local/coexpression prediction can be matched by technical or wrong-query information. Therefore C3's exact query-conditioned target, q-scalar exclusion, wrong-q, query-agnostic, address-only and technical+q falsifiers must remain intact.

### 5.4 C5 regulatory specialist

In 1,860 immune/microglia-like cells from the safe 50k corpus, simple +2 SD tail probes show strong source shifts despite both genes being structurally addressable across all 42 operators.

STAT1:

- overall tail 4.25%, 15/85 donors;
- HVS 0.78%; NPH52 25.66%; SEA_AD 0%.

SPI1:

- overall tail 7.69%, 42/85 donors;
- HVS 16.41%; NPH52 33.22%; SEA_AD 1.47%.

These source shifts may reflect real biology, measurement sensitivity/depth, or both. They are not explained by simple structural gene absence. This supports the existing C5 contract: source/operator/depth matching, same-nucleus multimodal recoverability classification and causal restraint are mandatory.

### 5.5 Anonymous orthogonal factors

On the small frozen 84-cell cross-source truth table, top-3 PCA/orthogonal program-score subspaces differed materially between pooled and source-specific fits. Largest principal angles were ~88 degrees HVS, ~62 degrees NPH52 and ~83 degrees SEA_AD.

The sample is too small for an inferential claim. It is enough to reject the assumption that an anonymous orthogonal factor automatically becomes a portable biological object.

## 6. Candidate implications

### C1_SHARED_EMERGENT

Keep active and unchanged conceptually.

Robust implementation requirement:

- shared/global target is a **stable accountable block/subspace**, not a collection of semantically named coordinates;
- must beat source/operator/depth/support shortcuts;
- must preserve eligible rare/common biology;
- donor/source/technology transport is explicit;
- uncertainty separates biological evidence from measurement quality.

### C2_RELATIONAL_SHARED

Keep active under the current replay/synthetic rules.

Do not convert C2 into an 'OPF relational factor'. Relational biology is defined by relational estimands and wrong-cell/scale/null behavior, not by occupying an orthogonal latent direction.

If C2 qualifies, its target may be implemented as a block whose internal coordinates/factors are numerical choices. Its biology remains accountable through the relational gate.

### C3_QUERY_MEASURED

Keep active with the exact query-safety contract.

Query conditioning should be structural: query identity/descriptor may route the predictor, but the student's q scalar remains excluded where frozen. Correct query-specific increment must beat q-count, q-address, query-agnostic, wrong-q and technical+q controls.

An anonymous global factorization does not replace this.

### C6_SHARED_QUERY_COMPOSITE

C6 is an implementation/composition candidate after C1+C3 qualify.

Its purpose is not 'more factors'. It must preserve both constituent claims and prove incremental efficiency/coherence without biological loss. Aggregate JEPA loss cannot compensate for a failed C1 or C3 subclaim.

### C4_STRUCTURED_COMPOSITE

C4 remains C1+C3+C2 accountability in one possible implementation. It is eligible only if constituents qualify, and relational increment should be tested against C6 rather than rewarded for component count.

### C5_REGULATORY_SPECIALIST

Keep specialist-only. A privileged RNA+ATAC teacher may improve C5 target construction, but student targets must be partitioned into:

- RNA-recoverable point target;
- predictable-but-stochastic/distributional target;
- teacher-private privileged state;
- not estimable.

No deterministic student loss on teacher-private ATAC realization.

## 7. Architectures considered

### A. Global OPF over one monolithic biological target

Strengths:

- simple;
- good numerical conditioning and synthesis;
- dedicated predictive pathways can reduce capacity conflict.

Major robustness problems here:

- can rotate C1/C3/C2 information across anonymous factors while preserving loss;
- factor activity is a marginal-variance criterion, not a rare-biology guarantee;
- constituent failure scope becomes hard to audit;
- biological overlap between shared/query/relational blocks is forced into geometric orthogonality without scientific justification.

Decision: **do not use as the default biological architecture.** Keep as a capacity-matched architecture control later if desired.

### B. Blockwise OPF

C1, C3, C2 and specialist targets remain separate; OPF is used only inside each qualified block.

Strengths:

- preserves block accountability;
- may improve capacity allocation inside a high-dimensional stable block;
- easier to compare with standard multi-head prediction.

Risks:

- adds machinery before proving it is needed;
- rare/low-prevalence directions can still be disadvantaged by activity regularization;
- internal factors remain anonymous and must not receive biological names.

Decision: **reasonable optional architecture arm after target freeze, not the default assumption.**

### C. Conditional-information / sequential-innovation decomposition

Conceptually define C3 as information incremental over C1, C2 incremental over simpler blocks, etc., and train explicit conditional-residual/MI objectives.

Strengths:

- closely matches the scientific concept of non-redundant biological information.

Risks:

- neural mutual-information estimation is difficult and can itself choose an unstable dimensionality;
- sequential residualization is order-dependent;
- a noisy parent can push real shared biology into a child residual;
- extra loss terms create new optimization and calibration failure modes.

Decision: **use conditional increment as an evaluation/gate concept, not yet as the core training objective.**

### D. Typed target-portfolio JEPA — recommended default

This is mostly the architecture we had already converged toward, sharpened by the new paper and smoke tests.

The state is not one monolithic vector and not a set of semantically named orthogonal factors. It is an accountable portfolio:

`Z_target = [Z_shared, Z_query, Z_rel_if_qualified, Z_specialist_if_qualified, support, uncertainty]`

The implementation may fuse these internally, but every biological block retains an external target/gate identity.

Decision: **recommended robust pipeline.**

## 8. Recommended robust pipeline

### 8.1 Molecular ledger remains the evidence layer

Maintain the high-resolution molecular ledger/support state so target compression never becomes irreversible evidence erasure.

The ledger records:

- measured scalar/nonzero;
- measured zero;
- structurally unmeasured;
- unresolved collision where applicable;
- observation/operator support.

### 8.2 Observation operator is explicit and separate

Let `O_t` describe lawful physical measurement properties such as assay, chemistry, support, depth/detection and count-noise characteristics.

Do not give the biological state unrestricted donor, dataset or matrix identity.

Conceptually:

`biology z --O_t--> observation x`

and inference estimates biological state conditional on what observation process generated the evidence.

### 8.3 Target blocks are frozen before architecture tuning

Target discovery decides which of C1/C3/C2/C5-style blocks are scientifically qualified.

Architecture search is not allowed to redefine a failed block or invent a replacement target after training metrics are visible.

### 8.4 Shared student backbone, typed heads/adapters

Default student:

- one full-capacity shared RNA backbone;
- shared/global head if C1 qualifies;
- query-conditioned head if C3 qualifies;
- relational head if C2 is qualified and student-identifiable;
- regulatory/context specialist heads only when justified;
- separate support/uncertainty head.

Create separate full student models only after demonstrated negative transfer or incompatible evidence/abstention geometry.

### 8.5 Multiple teachers allowed, but share biology where appropriate

Teacher family:

- rich RNA shared teacher;
- query-conditioned rich RNA teacher;
- relational teacher only if C2 qualifies;
- privileged same-nucleus RNA+ATAC teacher for C5/regulatory characterization;
- optional spatial/context teacher where physically measured.

Overlapping teachers should use a shared biological core plus adapters/heads rather than independent unconstrained latent universes.

### 8.6 Recoverability is a first-class type

For every teacher target block classify information as:

1. deterministic/highly RNA-recoverable -> student point prediction;
2. predictable but stochastic -> distribution/uncertainty target;
3. teacher-private -> no deterministic student point loss;
4. NOT_ESTIMABLE -> unresolved.

This is a stronger pipeline boundary than forcing the student to imitate all teacher factors.

### 8.7 Stable-subspace losses, not coordinate worship

When the target object is stable only up to rotation, the loss/diagnostic should be subspace-aware. Biological interpretation remains at block/subspace level until coordinate stability is independently demonstrated.

A frozen runtime implementation may choose a convenient basis, but basis coordinates are not promoted to biological mechanisms by optimization alone.

### 8.8 Relational biology keeps relational supervision/evaluation

Do not expect a Euclidean latent factor to prove C2.

C2 should retain pair/triplet/neighborhood relational objectives or diagnostics where lawful, with matched wrong-cell controls, multiscale support and explicit NOT_ESTIMABLE states.

### 8.9 Query biology keeps query-specific supervision/evaluation

C3 retains:

- q-safe evidence;
- query descriptor routing;
- q-scalar exclusion where frozen;
- query-agnostic, wrong-q, q-count, q-address and technical+q falsifiers.

### 8.10 Rare biology uses exposure protection, not semantic orthogonality

Do not assume a low-variance rare program will earn an OPF factor.

Use:

- donor-aware hierarchical sampling;
- prospective minimum exposure floors for qualified rare regimes;
- empirical-prevalence evaluation;
- rare/common block-preservation gates;
- source/operator/measurement-quality matched controls.

Do not force a rare factor merely to satisfy a variance floor.

### 8.11 Uncertainty remains typed

Keep at least:

- biological evidence uncertainty;
- measurement uncertainty;
- measurement-domain support;
- biological-support novelty;
- block-specific abstention.

A rare but well-measured biological state must not be collapsed into technical OOD.

### 8.12 Optional numerical factorization only after target freeze

Within an already-qualified high-dimensional block, compare:

- single predictor;
- capacity-matched multi-head predictor;
- fixed orthogonal basis;
- learned OPF-style factorization.

Use identical data, backbone capacity, masking, optimizer budget and target block. A factorizer is retained only if it improves optimization/transport without degrading any mandatory biological regime.

No factor receives biological semantics from this comparison.

## 9. Training loss family

Do not define one universal scalar objective that can compensate across biological claims.

A practical training loss may be additive, but promotion remains gated:

`L = sum_b w_b L_predict,b + L_support/uncertainty + optional numerical regularizers`

where each active block `b` has its own predeclared evidence boundary.

Important:

- weights are optimization parameters, not biological importance scores;
- mandatory block failures cannot be rescued by a lower total loss;
- factor/activity regularizers, if used, are numerical health terms only;
- teacher-private components never enter deterministic student point loss.

## 10. Sampling and exposure

Use hierarchical bounded sampling:

`dataset/study -> donor -> cell`

with prospectively frozen weighting, rather than uniform cells or uniform datasets.

Add explicit exposure accounting for qualified rare regimes so rare programs are not starved by sheer cell-count imbalance. Exposure protection must not redefine their population prevalence in evaluation.

## 11. Qualification sequence

The robust pipeline is intentionally staged.

### Stage A — test the tests

- finish PR316 blinded synthetic known-truth qualification;
- negative/technical worlds must not yield biological support;
- true underpowered worlds must yield NOT_ESTIMABLE;
- fine failure must not erase meso/rare/other claims.

### Stage B — qualify target portfolio

- exact C1/C3 receipts;
- C2 if active;
- cross-cutting rare/common/source/operator controls;
- C6/C4 only after constituents;
- C5 specialist independently;
- explicit portfolio complementarity/non-redundancy/interference.

### Stage C — architecture tournament on frozen targets

Compare only implementations of the same frozen target portfolio:

1. shared backbone + standard typed heads (default baseline);
2. shared backbone + capacity-matched multi-head branches;
3. optional blockwise OPF;
4. only if justified, separate full students for demonstrated interference.

Do not let architecture results redefine the target.

### Stage D — privileged multimodal reconciliation

After RNA target freeze:

- same-nucleus RNA+ATAC;
- Morabito donor-level replication;
- internal SEA-AD corroboration;
- SCENIC/motif evidence within claim limits;
- classify privileged increment as recoverable/stochastic/private.

### Stage E — production training authority

Only after target and architecture qualification, CUDA/packing/EMA mechanics and independent review.

## 12. What not to add now

Do **not** add by default:

- global biological orthogonality;
- mutual-information neural estimators as deciding target losses;
- separate students for every block;
- a learned dataset-ID biological embedding;
- hard semantic meaning for OPF coordinates;
- a universal rare-factor variance floor;
- reconstruction of teacher-private privileged state;
- pathology/disease optimization during foundation target/architecture selection.

These all create more ways to obtain apparently good metrics while weakening scientific interpretability and transfer.

## 13. Main recommendation

The robust default is:

**molecular ledger + explicit observation operator -> shared RNA backbone -> biologically typed target heads -> recoverability-aware teacher/student targets -> typed uncertainty/support**, with optional numerical factorization *inside* already-qualified blocks only when a capacity-matched test proves it helps.

In compact form:

`measured molecular ledger`
`        + observation operator`
`                 |`
`                 v`
`          shared RNA encoder`
`          /      |      \\`
`   shared     query     relational(if qualified)`
`      |         |             |`
` stable block  q-safe head   relational head`
`          \\      |      /`
`        support + typed uncertainty`
`                 |`
`       optional qualified specialists`

This architecture is intentionally less flashy than inventing a new universal factorization. Its strength is that every biologically meaningful component has an explicit evidence source, recoverability class, falsifier set, failure scope and transfer requirement.

## 14. Relationship to JEPA-Anything

JEPA-Anything should be retained as:

- evidence that factorized predictive paths can improve optimization;
- an implementation baseline/optional within-block factorizer;
- a reminder to audit capacity allocation and synthesis conditioning.

It should **not** become:

- the target-discovery mechanism;
- evidence that factors are biological mechanisms;
- a replacement for rare/query/relational qualification;
- a reason to force orthogonality between biological blocks.

The correct project question is therefore not 'can we exceed JEPA-Anything?' but:

> Can we train a predictive cellular state whose biological claims remain valid under donor/source/technology transfer, rare-state stress, query and relational falsifiers, privileged-evidence restrictions, uncertainty separation and known-truth test qualification?

That is the pipeline this audit recommends.
