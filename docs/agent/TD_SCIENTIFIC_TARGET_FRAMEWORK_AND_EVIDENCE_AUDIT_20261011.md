## 2. Re-audit of the historical relational program

### TD56S — what actually survived

TD56S prospectively compared two disjoint 512-gene Molecular Ledger views using fixed within-view gene-pair order coordinates. Geometry was evaluated inside donor×operator strata; donor was the primary unit; the null reassigned Y-cell identities inside donor×operator depth/detection blocks.

All three source families passed. The observed median-Fisher donor correlations were approximately 0.798 HVS, 0.801 NPH52 and 0.878 SEA_AD, each above its matched-null p95. Independent hash-pair subsets and the pair-ordering ambiguity audit also passed.

**Scientific implication:** disjoint molecular evidence contains a strongly recurring within-donor relational object. TD56S does *not* establish a universal coordinate system, production dimensionality, cross-source absolute scale, partial-evidence student predictability, or training authority.

### TD57B — what the prospective replication added

TD57B used two new, disjoint gene panels and scale-free anchored triplet ordering. Donor was primary; matched nulls preserved donor/operator/depth/detection. It passed 24/24 cases across HVS, NPH52, SEA_AD and both panels. The weakest observed-minus-null-p95 margin was `+0.0375178074843`; all 24 also exceeded the maximum matched null.

**Scientific implication:** the recurring object is not merely pooled-donor structure and does not require a universal absolute distance scale. The strongest historical claim is therefore about **scale-free donor-recurrent relational order**, not fixed coordinates.

### TD57C — the fine-local falsifier must remain binding

TD57C prospectively asked whether a Z-selected nearest-one-third neighborhood retained X/Y relational ordering. Panel-0 HVS failed 2/4 donor-half cases and the sequential experiment stopped. NPH52, SEA_AD and Panel 1 were not opened.

A post-failure same-X/Y unrestricted forensic diagnostic passed 4/4. That diagnostic cannot rescue TD57C; instead it localizes the failure: the molecular views retained broad relational signal, while aggressive nearest-third localization removed enough recurrent structure to fail the frozen screen.

**Scientific implication:** deepest/fine local mining cannot be the primary target merely because global relational structure is strong.

### TD59 — mesoscale support, not a production neighborhood constant

TD59 prospectively tested a broader nearest-half mesoscale relation on fresh molecular views. It passed 24/24 cases under its frozen p95 criterion; the weakest margin was `+0.0011566307718604563`. Twenty-two of 24 cases exceeded null maximum; two HVS cases did not.

Because TD57C and TD59 used different prospectively frozen views, their outcomes bracket an operational locality frontier but do not identify a universal transition at one-third versus one-half. The 50k locality-fraction search is closed.

**Scientific implication:** broad/mesoscale affinity or rank structure remains scientifically plausible; hard-coded nearest-third is falsified; nearest-half is not a production hyperparameter.

## 3. Re-audit of the historical learned-target failure modes

The August 24 calibration bundle provides a critical warning against promoting a target merely because it is stable, learnable, noncollapsed, or easy to optimize.

### Address/operator decomposition

For the historical token-level H representation, address plus operator explain essentially all variance:

| checkpoint / representation | address EV | operator increment | address+operator EV | residual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| u0 rich_H | 0.997592 | 0.001791 | 0.999384 | 0.000616 |
| u0 partial_H | 0.998173 | 0.001328 | 0.999501 | 0.000499 |
| u205 rich_H | 0.998224 | 0.001321 | 0.999545 | 0.000455 |
| u205 partial_H | 0.999493 | 0.000365 | 0.999858 | 0.000142 |

This means a representation can appear extraordinarily stable while being dominated by address identity and measurement/operator structure.

### Synthetic mechanism stress

In the historical balanced-vs-empirical synthetic stress test, JEPA loss fell to approximately `8e-4` by update 500, while the planted biology-factor R² remained negative. Source balanced accuracy remained 1.0 and support-count R² remained about 0.995–0.997.

**Binding conclusion:** optimization success, low loss, variance/covariance health, effective rank, target stability, or student-teacher cosine are not biological qualification. Every future target tournament needs explicit address/support/operator baselines and a matched value/support null.

## 4. What R5 repaired in the teacher/student formulation

The R5 developmental tournament correctly identified a conceptual failure in earlier work: the purported R4 teacher and student were both proxy predictors trained on the same 700 other genes and neither directly constructed the measured target.

R5 instead defined a real measured teacher object. For cell `c`, query `q`, and training-donor-selected non-q context `N(q)`, the teacher target was the independently normalized same-cell non-q UMI vector:

`a_T(c,q)[j] = log1p(10000 * k_c,N(q)[j] / sum(k_c,N(q)))`.

The student received a complementary masked RNA panel plus the query address, not the query scalar. The target normalization excluded q. Unsupported/empty target panels abstained; measured zero remained measured.

Within the reused developmental population, q-specific prediction beat generic and technical baselines, and a teacher-visible q scalar added a small amount of information to non-q context but was a poor substitute for it. These results are not current target authority, but they establish a cleaner information-asymmetry contract.

**Binding conclusion:** a future teacher must construct state from evidence genuinely richer than the student's evidence. Query identity can condition a head; query value must not become a hidden student channel. A q-visible teacher is permissible only with a mandatory q-excluded ablation and a primary non-q measured anchor.

## 5. Multimodal/regulatory evidence: allowed role and forbidden overclaim

The existing Stage75/76/81 evidence is scientifically useful but weaker than a validated regulatory teacher target.

Current project evidence establishes:

- Stage75 integrated evidence contains 96 candidate TF–target rows across 27 target symbols and 7 motif-supported regulators.
- Tier A direct motif support: STAT1, ELF1, SPI1.
- Tier B extended-only motif support: IRF8, BACH1, CEBPA, RELA.
- Stage75 peak–gene evidence includes nearest-gene/proximity assumptions and is explicitly not a validated eRegulon/GRN or causal mapping.
- Stage81A1 classifies the Stage75 graph as a **soft regulatory-prior candidate** after vocabulary/control gates, not causal truth.
- GSE174367 microglia snRNA is allowed for coactivity provenance/context, not clean external validation.
- GSE174367 microglia snATAC is allowed for chromatin-support annotation, not causal enhancer validation.
- Cross-source donor/specimen identity is not fully resolved in the Stage81A1 inventory.
- Perturbation assets exist, but their assignments/controls/replicate units/normalization still require dedicated contracts before causal use.

**Binding conclusion:** ATAC/SCENIC+/regulatory evidence should enter the next target program as prospectively frozen **falsifiers, support annotations, or optional specialist channels**, not as privileged biological truth. Any relation derived from Morabito RNA/ATAC cannot be independently validated by reusing the same derived association.

## 6. Observation process must be separated from biology

The historical corpus work supports a cleaner model:

`z_biology --O_t--> X_observed`

and inference:

`q(z_biology | X_observed, O_t)`.

`O_t` may contain physically meaningful acquisition descriptors such as scRNA/snRNA, platform/chemistry, measured vocabulary, sequencing depth, detected-gene characteristics and count-split noise estimates. It must not become a free dataset ID, arbitrary matrix embedding, or donor ID.

A target should be approximately invariant to measurement realization/noise when biology is held comparable, but remain sensitive to genuine cell state, tissue/region, donor biology and age-related biology.

## 7. Coverage audit and design consequence

The August support ledger contains 41,238 molecular addresses:

- 289 measured in no source family;
- 10,334 measured in one source family;
- 13,269 measured in two source families;
- 17,346 measured in all three source families;
- 30,615 measured in at least two source families;
- 17,186 scalar-measured in all 42 historical operators.

The fit104 source population is also highly imbalanced (about 4.12M SEA_AD, 236k NPH52, 199k HVS), while the historical T1 training cache was strongly HVS-enriched.

A single target that requires every modality/address on every cell would either discard most of the corpus or manufacture missing biology. Conversely, a single pooled objective would allow the largest source to define success.

**Coverage framework:**

1. A **universal shared/relational component** must be definable from lawful RNA evidence across the broad corpus and evaluated donor-first/source-explicitly.
2. A **query-local component** is active only when its support contract is satisfied; otherwise it abstains.
3. A **regulatory/chromatin specialist component** is optional and may only activate where independent provenance/support is sufficient; absence is abstention, not imputed negative biology.
4. A **measurement/support channel** remains separate from biological target state.
5. Discovery and eventual training sampling must be hierarchical and bounded across dataset/source → donor → cell, not raw cell-uniform.

This maximizes coverage without pretending all cells carry identical evidence.

## 8. Candidate target architecture — scientific incumbent, not winner

The strongest current scientific design is a structured protocol rather than one monolithic coordinate vector:

`T(c,q) = { Z_shared(c), R_broad/meso(c), Z_query(c,q), Support(c,q), U_bio(c,q), U_meas(c,q), optional Specialist(c,q) }`

### `Z_shared(c)` — emergent shared biological state

A learned teacher state may remain coordinate-emergent. Its authority comes from frozen invariants and falsifiers, not named axes. It must survive donor-balanced resampling, disjoint molecular views, and measurement attacks.

### `R_broad/meso(c)` — relational constraint

The representation must preserve scale-free donor-local relational ordering supported by TD56/TD57B and broad/mesoscale structure consistent with TD59. No nearest-third primary loss is allowed. No production nearest-half constant is implied.

### `Z_query(c,q)` — measured q-conditioned contextual anchor

A q-conditioned component should be anchored to measured non-q same-cell context under independent normalization. Query address may condition the head. Query scalar must be absent from student evidence and q-derived normalization; q-visible teacher and q-excluded teacher must both be tested.

### `Support(c,q)`

Physical measurability, structural support and collision state are explicit. Unsupported target components abstain. Measured zero remains measured.

### `U_bio(c,q)` and `U_meas(c,q)`

Biological uncertainty should be tied to an **evidence-response curve**: how much inferred state changes as lawful molecular evidence increases. Measurement uncertainty should be tied separately to a **depth/remeasurement curve** for the same evidence. These should not be collapsed into one generic OOD score.

### Optional specialist component

Chromatin/regulatory or other specialist state is permitted only when independently supportable and prospectively qualified. It is not required for universal coverage.

## 9. Target-winner promotion criteria

A future target family is not a winner merely because one metric is positive. Promotion requires all applicable gates below.

1. **Corrected-substrate integrity** — G6/G7 must first pass under explicit owner authority.
2. **Corrected replay consistency** — frozen TD56/57B/57C/59 corrected replay must be audited under the predeclared interpretation rules.
3. **Donor-primary recurrence** — effects must survive held-out/resampled donors; repeated queries/cells are not biological replicates.
4. **Source replication** — HVS, NPH52 and SEA_AD are adjudicated explicitly; pooled success cannot hide source failure.
5. **Matched shortcut defeat** — beat technical-only, address/support/operator, wrong-cell and value/support-preserving nulls.
6. **Nontrivial teacher construction** — target contains measured/relational biological information not reducible to q scalar, source, operator or static address identity.
7. **Student information asymmetry** — student predicts from strictly poorer lawful evidence; no q/value/normalization/QC leakage.
8. **Evidence-response behavior** — more lawful biological evidence should improve/stabilize state in a predeclared manner.
9. **Measurement robustness** — remeasurement/depth variation should not be confused with biological novelty.
10. **Transfer hierarchy** — held-out donor, then matrix, then study/dataset, then technology where scientifically possible.
11. **Coverage and calibrated abstention** — report eligible coverage; never turn unsupported components into fabricated values.
12. **Learned-teacher continuity** — if a learned/EMA teacher is used, its relational geometry must reproduce across disjoint lawful views and beat matched wrong-cell attacks before student performance can promote it.
13. **No post-outcome target rescue** — thresholds, locality fractions, modality roles and nulls are frozen before outcome access.

## 10. Representation discovery and training remain separate questions

The program must preserve four distinct layers:

1. **Biological-object discovery:** is there a recurrent biological object worth predicting?
2. **Teacher construction:** can richer lawful evidence construct that object without shortcut dominance?
3. **Student predictability:** can restricted evidence predict the teacher object above technical/null baselines?
4. **JEPA optimization:** can a model learn the frozen teacher/student protocol without collapse or shortcut substitution?

Success at a later layer cannot backfill failure at an earlier layer. In particular, low JEPA loss cannot establish biological-object validity.

## 11. Current scientific ruling

### Rejected as primary target designs

- address/operator-dominated H-token targets;
- hidden-gene scalar reconstruction presented as world-state learning;
- q scalar imputation disguised as query conditioning;
- SCENIC+/ATAC candidate graphs treated as validated causal teacher truth;
- pooled-cell success dominated by SEA_AD;
- forced multimodal completion for unsupported cells.

### Scientific incumbent to test prospectively

A **structured, multiscale target protocol** combining:

- emergent shared state;
- broad/mesoscale scale-free relational continuity;
- measured q-conditioned non-q contextual anchors;
- explicit support/abstention;
- separate biological and measurement uncertainty;
- optional independently qualified specialist channels.

This is a **candidate architecture**, not a target winner.
