## Real TRAIN finding

Read-only analysis of the authenticated FOUNDATION 50K discovery expression sample isolated 665 microglia-like cells across 66 TRAIN donors and all three sources (HVS, NPH52, SEA-AD). After donor aggregation and within-source centering, substantial donor-structured residual expression remained, consistent with the earlier exact-S174 result that donor effects survive measurement/source adjustment.

Three candidate rare motifs emerged from high-loading residual genes:

- `IRF5_IFIT1`
- `CR1_S100A12`
- `SIGLEC15_NINJ1`

At a within-source +2 SD cell-score threshold they recurred across 19, 16, and 15 donors respectively, and each appeared in all three sources. No single donor contributed more than 20% of any tail.

## Critical negative result

These motifs did **not** show stable within-source gene coherence outside HVS. Source-stratified median pairwise gene correlations were near zero or negative in NPH52 and SEA-AD, and independent source-specific PCA showed essentially no overlap among the top residual genes: across PCs 1–6, the maximum top-30 overlap between any two sources was one gene; zero PC pairs shared two or more genes.

Therefore:

- donor-structured rare residual biology is supported as a phenomenon;
- these exact motifs are candidate leads only;
- none should be promoted as a biological target from this analysis alone;
- target discovery should test them against stronger regulatory, multiomic, spatial, perturbational, and corrected-replay evidence.
