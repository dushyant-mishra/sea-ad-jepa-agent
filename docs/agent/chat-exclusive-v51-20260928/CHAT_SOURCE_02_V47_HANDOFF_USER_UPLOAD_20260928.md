### Conditional-composition fallback

This is developmental, not qualified. Local synthetic work found that both simple conditional composition and donor-level binomial/logistic formulations can false-positive severely under gene-specific differential capture. In the local stress tests, observed-depth adjustment did not solve the constructed differential-capture nuisance; an oracle hidden-capture covariate did. Therefore composition requires a differential-capture negative arm before any biological interpretation.

Same-nucleus binomial thinning is only a **postcapture measurement-robustness diagnostic**. It cannot identify or reproduce the original hidden biochemical capture process.

### Relational target lane — critical new audit

TD56–TD59 remain useful evidence that disjoint molecular views carry recurrent cell-state geometry across donors and sources, but their historical biological-specificity null is not sufficient for training authority. TD57B's wrong-cell null reassigns Y identities within donor×operator depth/detection blocks. That destroys both true cell identity and any residual same-cell technical state not captured by those blocks.

The local relational-control audit performed an exact NPH52 TD57B Panel-0 replay and then adversarial synthetic controls. A technical-only shared latent state, with no biological latent, can beat the historical matched wrong-cell null. A clean independent null fails and a planted shared-state positive passes, showing that the gate has sensitivity but does not uniquely attribute the signal to biology.

**Consequence:** TD56–TD59 do not currently authorize scientific teacher training. They should not be discarded; their donor-primary, source-separated, disjoint-view structure is reusable. The null must be strengthened.

## Training status

Scientific teacher training requires, in order:

1. a target with a credible negative control that survives the successor technical-state gate;
2. a positive-control/sensitivity arm;
3. authenticated production reader/feature-axis availability and q-safe normalization;

Student distillation and broad production training come only after the teacher survives its prospective qualification and independent biological evaluation.
