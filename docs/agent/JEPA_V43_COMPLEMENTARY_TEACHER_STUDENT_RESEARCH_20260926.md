## 0. The scientific target comes before architecture

The desired model predicts a **query-address-conditioned, non-scalar, same-cell molecular state** using lawful remaining RNA. A teacher team is not automatically a faithful world model. Each teacher must expose what it represents, which original measurements support that representation, what remains absent/uncertain and what independent evidence could disprove it. A student predicts **only the information estimable from its own legal input**, not hidden experimental facts. Success requires meaningful shared and query-local biology beyond query identity, a measured gene's scalar, donor/source/technical identity or generic cell type. Observational molecular association is not evidence of perturbational causality.

**Correcting a premature design move:** never equate “teacher may see the queried scalar” with “student is allowed to leak it.” Preserve separately audited candidate teacher-input constructions, including full richer native support and query-sanitized complementary support; the teacher's best biological target is an empirical question. Before any selection, isolate indirect scalar reconstruction, q-normalization leakage and self-copying with planted positive/negative controls. Do not hard-code a q-excluded teacher solely to solve student leakage, and do not quietly promote an untested value-containing teacher as faithful biology.

## 3. Multi-teacher biological state and disagreement

Conceptual representation for cell c and query q:
`Z_T(c,q)=[z_shared(c,q), z_fine(c,q)|native_support, z_rare(c,q)|rare_support, u_bio, u_measurement, u_domain]`.
The vertical bars are explicit eligibility, **not** implicit zero filling. A trained alignment maps teachers to a comparability space **without averaging away specialist residuals**. A rare state can be rare within a donor and nevertheless require recurrence across independent donors for a cross-donor foundation claim; alternatively mark it cell-specific and uncertainty-limited rather than declare a false general finding. Never define rare state from protected pathology labels or post-hoc confirmation outcomes. The rare teacher may abstain if only technical rarity or a single donor supports a signal.

Keep the original measured molecular ledger intact and separate from predicted compressed representations. The observation model reports measured/unmeasured, operator, depth and other provenance; technical metadata may be used by a restricted observation-support/calibration branch but must not silently become the primary biological target. Test biological-evidence and sequencing-depth convergence separately; seek both state stability and honest uncertainty, not artificially high teacher consensus. Heterogeneous teacher disagreement can be signal, unsupported domain shift or failure and must be decomposed.
