## Critical historical precedence: positive/negative evidence together

**V1/V2/V3 graph era ≠ current masked-evidence teacher.** Older pathology OOF baselines, graph-controlled gene hypotheses and 2,957-feature representations cannot be compared directly to later masked, donor-held-out 41,238-address JEPA work. The Graph-JEPA scorecard already distinguished general internal prediction from graph-specific evidence and untouched external validation.

**Stage69 positive vs Stage63/Stage70 nonlock.** Historical rare microglia auxiliary results are worth retaining; DLPFC repeated-seed and within-donor shuffled nulls prevent a claim of robust unlocked pathology gains. The original rare-state path and later 14 annotated states/three de novo unstable neighborhoods are not interchangeable endpoints.

**Stage72–79 did proceed beyond acquisition.** TF–region–gene evidence, Tier-A in-silico perturbation and frozen archived GeneJEPA latent/graph comparisons were performed. *Stage79 graph-control* is not *V79 Q08 synthetic qualifier*; both stand, at their scopes, without causal or teacher authority.

It was superseded for current production dimension selection and is NOT today's approved model width. Neither `d_gene=160` nor historic `d_global=224` is a FULL104 numerical answer.

**TD19 prevents naive C2 overclaim.** Depth-only pseudo-states reproduce cross-source distance structure, while TD20–27 found narrower operator/source-conditional residuals; original TD56/57B/59 positives and TD57C negative must inherit those controls and must not be promoted into learned teacher recovery.

**Molecular identity mistakes are distinct.** A feature-to-address remapping is NOT a cell-row permutation, a BIN1 guide-display collision, PAR_Y crosswalk error, or a wrong-query comparator. Corrections apply per original estimator/receipt and do not invalidate all numerics indiscriminately. D1/CaseBundle issuer remains separately open.

**External projection and validation must not be conflated.** Morabito/Grubman/Rexach/Olah are already used/exposed within their historical protocols; weak or underpowered tests and adapted feature-space projections are still real work. They cannot become fresh unexposed validation by mentioning architecture changes.
