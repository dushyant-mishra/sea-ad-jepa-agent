## The peak-burden adjustment is doing real work

This is why V1 was superseded, and the numbers show it was necessary in the
right direction:

**Microglia has FEWER ATAC peaks than neuron — 50,199 against 55,932 — yet more
than twice the observed distal support (0.3426 vs 0.1467).** A raw support-rate
comparison would have been open to the objection that microglia simply has more
accessible genome; it does not, and the difference-in-differences removes the
question entirely.

Both comparators show a small but real positive excess (+0.034, +0.031): Nott
contacts do land in generally accessible regions more often than mirrored
positions do. That is expected and is exactly what the within-track null is for.
The microglial excess is roughly **5.5×** larger.

Four-way counts sharpen it. Against neuron, the observed asymmetry is
15,342 microglia-only against 3,046 neuron-only — **5.0×**. Under the null the
same asymmetry is only 6,408 against 3,829 — **1.7×**. The asymmetry is
contact-specific, not a standing property of the tracks.

## The finding that must travel with the pass

The contract mandated a local-density sensitivity precisely to "expose residual
dependence on genomic accessibility opportunity not eliminated by the within-track
null". It found some.

| local peak-density quartile | n | E_microglia | D_MN | D_MO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1 (lowest)** | 12,328 | **+0.0156** | **−0.0111** | **−0.0038** |
| Q2 | 16,115 | +0.1258 | +0.0859 | +0.0924 |
| Q3 | 13,526 | +0.2118 | +0.1680 | +0.1734 |
| **Q4 (highest)** | 20,784 | **+0.3258** | **+0.2981** | **+0.2945** |

**In the lowest-density quartile the microglial advantage is absent — both
difference-in-differences are slightly negative.** The effect rises
monotonically with local peak density and is roughly 20× larger in Q4 than Q1.

So the within-track null removed opportunity dependence in aggregate but **not
within strata**. The honest statement is:

> Nott microglial contacts show contact-specific microglial accessibility excess
> **in regions of moderate-to-high local peak density**. In the lowest-density
> quartile — about one fifth of the analysed population — there is no detectable
> microglial advantage over neuron or oligodendrocyte.

This is reported, not acted upon. The contract forbids trimming or reweighting on
these results, and no rescue question arises because the primary passed. But any
downstream use of this object inherits the gradient: a claim about a
low-density locus is not supported by this evidence even though the aggregate
gate passed.
