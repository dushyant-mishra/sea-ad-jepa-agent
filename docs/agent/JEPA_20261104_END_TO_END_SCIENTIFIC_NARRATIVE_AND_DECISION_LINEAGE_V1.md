## The project in one sentence

We set out to learn **biologically meaningful, transferable representations of human brain-cell states**, initially Alzheimer’s disease-associated microglia, that could eventually connect **RNA programs → tissue pathology → independent chromatin/regulatory/perturbational/spatial evidence → testable mechanistic hypotheses**. JEPA is the proposed *learning mechanism*, **not the biological endpoint**. Neither pathology prediction nor a low JEPA loss alone makes a biological representation or causal regulator valid.

## Act I — Alzheimer’s microglia and the first JEPA models (June 2026)

The initial project was deliberately biological and translational. SEA-AD Microglia-PVM single-nucleus RNA offered links to quantitative AT8/pTau, 6e10/Aβ, GFAP, Iba1, NeuN and other pathology measures. The question was whether the cells’ molecular programs—homeostatic, lipid/lysosomal/phagocytic, complement, antigen presentation, vascular/perivascular, inflammatory and plaque-associated—could explain variation across **held-out donors**, and support ranked biological hypotheses. That is more demanding than clusters, markers or attractive embeddings.

**V1, flat RNA-JEPA:** An RNA vector entered an encoder; an EMA teacher and predictor learned masked/module-masked latent targets. A historical pathology-aware v1 comparison reported donor-held-out AT8 Spearman about **0.497**, versus pseudobulk ridge about **0.422**. This was evidence of *internal prediction* under that historical setup, not a causal target or a pathology-blind foundation representation. Digital knockouts, latent Jacobians and adjusted effects produced hypotheses, but could not make an observational association an intervention result.

**V2/V2.1, graph JEPA:** Genes became nodes with expression and identity features; a 2,957-gene STRING prior supplied edges. The curriculum used CELLxGENE healthy/reference microglia (A), low-pathology SEA-AD adaptation (B), and full SEA-AD disease-context learning with anchor rehearsal (C). An overly rigid anchor prevented biological movement; a looser model collapsed into a narrow disease tube (effective dimensionality about **2.10**, top singular share about **0.821**). Elastic/projection-head variants improved bounded internal diagnostics but did not prove that real gene-network edges caused an improvement.

**V3 scientific scorecard:** The official donor-level out-of-fold five-pathology benchmark had module-mean baseline **0.3128** and a prespecified improvement threshold **0.3228**, with target-level safeguards. **Graph specificity was a separate scientific claim:** the real graph had to beat both no-graph and strict shuffled graph at matched conditions. A model could predict pathology without learning legitimate graph topology. Historic Morabito, Grubman, Rexach and Olah projections were *actually performed*, but small cohorts, source feature differences, prior exposure, low power and weak held-out associations prevented treating them as pristine independent confirmation.

**What this act changed:** Prediction, graph-specificity, external transfer, mechanistic validity and causal effect became distinct levels of evidence. This is the origin of the project’s strict claim ladder, not a recent bureaucratic addition.

## Act II — Rare-cell states, candidate genes and independent regulatory biology (June–August)

The project did not abandon Alzheimer’s biology when graph prediction proved fragile. It tested whether **rare microglial states** and regional donor-tail effects carried information missed by broad cell-state averages. Stage53–70 evaluated MTG and DLPFC high-tail states, auxiliary heads, donor-level modules and strong nulls. Stage69 had a bounded internal rare-auxiliary positive (**0.35910** mean proxy, **+0.03240** versus Stage27C), but Stage70’s repeated-seed/benchmark-lock criteria failed. Stage71 graph-auxiliary and Stage73R network priors did not demonstrate superiority to shuffled controls. These are important *negative architecture priors*, not evidence rare biology does not exist.

The parallel Alzheimer’s candidate program (**Stages36–49**) assembled **103** knowledge resources and **770** annotations, resolved **18** traceable candidate genes across four biological mechanisms, produced mechanism dossiers, PI review material, assay priorities and manuscript/methods work. The original Stage48 explicitly ruled out repeating a generic whole-gene rediscovery absent a new biological question. This scientific/translation program remains real work, not simply a gene list.

**Regulatory and perturbation program:** The team incorporated RNA, snATAC, motif/cisTarget and SCENIC+-related evidence; Stage72B constructed **541** candidate coactivity edges; Stage75F preserved direct/extended/no-motif support tiers for named TFs. Stages76–79 (the **original GeneJEPA biological perturbation lineage**, distinct from later synthetic V79) actually ran a **frozen 2,957-feature** model through **53 Tier-A signed TF–target edges**, **12** perturbation scenarios and **152 real/control graph variants**. Digital changes to input expression are *model perturbations*, not experimental drug, CRISPR or enhancer perturbations. Ten regulator directions remained unresolved, and graph-control software success was not evidence of validated regulatory causality.

Actual external perturbation assets and druggability sources were also investigated: an eight-study/16-asset inventory, guide/feature validation, Day8/target work, independent **179,384-row** clone-aware bulk recomputation and a **negative CRISPRbrain eligibility** finding (only **1/31** joint target engagements; cross-screen concordance near chance). Later seven-track public E2G evidence survey was **executed**, but contained no microglia-specific causal enhancer validation. Morabito/Grubman/Rexach/Olah and spatial/Slide-tags/Fang experiments had different source/validation scopes. Their completed negatives must not be erased; their exploratory positives must not become intervention proof.

**What this act changed:** A single expression/STRING graph and internal pathology benchmark were not enough to prove a microglial mechanism. Regulatory support, perturbational reliability, external biological unit, and genuine *physical* spatial context required their own evidence and controls. The long-term aim remains *biological discovery with independently testable hypotheses*, not RNA-only prediction as the final product.

## Act III — From disease-tuned graph model to a general molecular foundation (August–September)

The next shift was larger than choosing a better loss. The project sought an **accountable biological state across heterogeneous observations**, capable of retaining gene-local, common, rare, source-conditional and context-dependent biology without learning merely tissue, source, cell type or sequencing-depth identity.

**Molecular identity:** Stage81A2/A2R and subsequent source audits distinguished gene symbol from molecular address and retained exact/legacy/anchored provenance. The historical 41,238-address universal ledger comprised **40,422 current + 773 legacy + 43 anchored** identities; **2,010** additional ambiguous or identifier-poor identities were preserved outside unrestricted identity. The **42 operators** can return measured nonzero, **measured zero**, structurally unmeasured or collision-unresolved observations. Gene aliases and source-feature collisions are *observation rights*, not biological synonyms. Later #509/#513 closed HVS/SEA-AD off-ledger feature accounting and reused the existing 322-entry ETL ledger; NPH52 symbol-only and source-selection issuance remained restricted. Source equality is not automatically independent validation.

**The data scale changed:** The older frozen foundation was **13 datasets/36 matrices/149 TRAIN donors**, with 4,096 addresses in some development experiments; a later FULL104 program accounted **4,553,407 cells, 8,915 Level-4 blocks, 104 donors and 41,238 addresses** under its own split and estimand. Those are different populations/versions, not conflicting alternative counts. The original bounded 4K state, PCA160, a historical one-SE **224D Freeze1**, later full-stream D_shared/D_private/D_obs selectors and 160-wide network tokens are also **different things**. A token width is model capacity, not a biological dimension estimate. The full-stream shared-D investigation had a documented negative `TEACHER_BIOLOGY_LIMIT` outcome under its original gate; technical dimension-selector implementation did not select a new universal biological width.

**Architecture changed:** The 24-slot Perceiver compression lost gene-local information. A more accountable **per-gene Molecular Ledger** (original 4,096 gene tokens × 160 width; eventual broader address registry) preserved gene identity/local structure, with separate **global cell**, **gene/query-local**, **program** and **combined structured** readout families. A global cell embedding cannot automatically stand in for biologically relevant gene-local state. Rotating latent coordinate axes can make a trained model appear to have lost molecular information even when the *subspace* retains it; comparing individual coordinates is not enough.

**Observation physics:** The conceptual model became `biological state z -> assay/operator O_t -> observed X`. Real heterogeneous data do not directly supply a clean latent z. The frozen 4,096-gene observation audit found one structural feature-mask pattern across its then-registered matrices, but that does **not** mean technologies have equivalent depth, capture efficiency, cell/nucleus composition or observation physics, nor does it apply to all 41,238 addresses. Later real u205 source/operator probes still read source and technical support nearly perfectly (e.g. source balanced accuracy **1.0**, support-count R² **0.9998**); technology invariance was not established. Donor, arbitrary dataset ID and pathology were forbidden as unrestricted biological shortcuts. Measurement, domain, biological and context uncertainty must remain distinguishable; a learned Bayesian/RBB belief model *actually failed* to preserve intrinsic information under training, so a probabilistic posterior cannot be assumed qualified.

**What this act changed:** The foundation is not an attempt to force every dataset into one fabricated complete RNA vector. It is an attempt to represent **biology under measured, unequal and sometimes non-identifiable evidence**, with genomic identity, denominator, donor and assay physics explicitly accounted for.

## Act IV — The real target failure: information in the teacher versus information available to the student

The project then confronted a conceptual limitation of JEPA, not merely a bad checkpoint. A **teacher** may see richer lawful molecular measurements; a **student** sees only a permitted partial view (possibly a gene query, a lower-depth count subset, a less complete assay, or contextual evidence). What should the student learn? A teacher state is scientifically useful only if it **retains real biology** and its *predictable component* is recoverable from what the student is allowed to observe. Teacher-private variation may require a distributional target, an auxiliary critic or `NOT_ESTIMABLE`, not fabricated exact point prediction.

The observed failures narrowed this question:

- Old **Stage81A3**: ten 500-update synthetic JEPA trajectories could reduce loss strongly while failing to recover planted masked biological factors. Low loss is not biological information.
- Six-IPB follow-up: the original eight planned trajectories stopped after **three completed**, not an eight-run success/failure. Later **Stage81B** compared CURRENT, JEPA_ONLY and ANCHOR_ONLY at u100/u300/u500. Hidden-gene/CELL **anchor harmed** intrinsic gene identity (~**7.618×** more displacement than JEPA_ONLY). An aligned frozen reader later recovered information missed by rotating online probes. Do not confuse the earlier anchor with the later teacher/student evidence-rights separation.
- T0 arithmetic block mean erased local/rare content; T1 gene-level and TCTX query-self-masked targets held more local information, but three corrected short-horizon T0/T1/TCTX comparisons did **not** produce a target winner. Richer teacher targets can still be **conditionally unpredictable**; original Signed-V2 illustrates that failure.
- Real forward-only foundation tests (no encoder updates) found that high apparent target-row R² was largely explained by **gene/address identity**. Core-only evidence missed harder/rare information; lawful core+halo/distributed evidence was important. Donor-cross-fitted per-address residualization had already been attempted and failed to supply an adequate molecular target.
- Historical 41K T1 u205 optimizer had **48** dead attention-related Adam-moment tensors despite falling loss, attributable to a specific precision/backward problem. The F1-B synthetic repair demonstrated healthy gradient mechanics, *not* biological target selection.
- Rich teacher candidates (query-value-aware, value-blind, block-blind, complementary-teacher team, shared typed heads) were designed; a corrected synthetic T_A/T_B comparison was **NOT INFORMATIVE** about real target choice. V44 donor-heldout PCA/ridge proxy gave a limited shared-multihead positive prior, not a learned EMA winner. R5 tested a genuine **eight other measured genes**, query-excluded same-cell target, with historically reconciled held-donor q-aware R² **0.4300** versus technical+q **0.3496** and no-q **0.2760**. Its original decisive receipt was not directly reopened in this narrative; it was *already run* and does not prove every query or C3 learned student.

**What this act changed:** The model must **not** be trained first and congratulated on low loss. It must define an identifiable, admissible target and distinguish gene identity, technical/global shortcuts, recoverable biology and legitimately unobservable teacher information. For a learned target, emergent geometry may be an acceptable representation; an arbitrary fixed coordinate tensor is not the only scientific option.

## Act V — Target discovery becomes a biological competition, NOT “C2 versus C3” (September–October)

Real RNA showed that some **within-cell relational order** was reproducible across donors and disjoint measured gene panels. TD19 also showed why that could mislead: **depth-only artificial cell states** almost reproduced HVS–SEA-AD source geometry (**r≈0.980–0.986**), and proposed universal same-gene dependency transport failed. TD20–27 had narrower source-conditional signals. TD56 and TD57B prospectively tested donor-recurrent broad/global relational ordering; **TD57B 24/24** cases passed against wrong-cell controls matched on donor/operator/depth/detection. TD57C's **nearest-third** fine-local criterion failed in HVS. TD59 **nearest-half mesoscale 24/24** passed its p95-null gate, but not all beats against the strict null maximum and not every locality fraction is justified. PR #314 **executed one corrected real replay**, confirming this broad/mesoscale-positive/fine-negative scale bracket. This is an important **measured-RNA biological relationship**, not learned JEPA geometry, source-invariant causal biology or a winning target.

The project explicitly made the **full target lattice**:

| Candidate | Biological object and dependency |
| --- | --- |
| **C1 — Shared emergent** | General shared biological state learned from measurements; not eliminated by C2-specific failure. |
| **C2 — Relational shared** | Donor-recurrent between-cell/within-cell relational geometry. |
| **C3 — Query measured** | Gene/query-conditioned context from measured complementary biology, with wrong-query and scalar-leak controls. |
| **C6 — Shared + query** | Composite requiring independently admissible C1 and C3. |
| **C4 — Shared + relational + query** | Composite requiring C2 and C3; its *increment* is compared to an **independently qualified C6**, otherwise not estimable. |
| **C5 — Regulatory specialist** | Regulatory/modalities specialist; cannot rescue an unqualified foundation target merely by adding a network prior. |

PR #275 froze the routing and zero/one/multiple/unresolved survivor law. PR #313 built an outcome-blind executor framework. PR #338 prospectively designed **28 real scientific gates**; **15** were partially specified and **13** remained scientifically blocked. No C1–C6 winner was selected. Crucially, relational results govern **C2 and the relational portion of C4**; they do not eliminate **C1, C3, C6**, and do not make C5 biology invalid or proven.

Representation *form* is a **separate** question from target *family*: global cell state, gene/query-local, program-level and structured state remain alternatives. A given target can potentially support several biological readouts, and none inherits causal regulatory authority by name.

**What this act changed:** The proper scientific object is a contest between explicitly defined and falsifiable **kinds of biological information**, rather than a one-lane search for an easy reconstruction target. An appropriate terminal can be **no candidate**, **several incomparable candidates**, or **not estimable**, not forced winner selection.

## Act VI — What the synthetic work actually did, and what it did NOT do

Synthetic data were **not one dataset** and not a stand-in for the Alzheimer’s patients. They were deliberately different laboratories for different failure modes:

1. **Planted biological information and model behavior:** Original Stage81A3/A3R/Stage81B, IPB gene-local/core-halo/rare/factor worlds and the synthetic F1-B gradient bridge tested what a model could retain or lose when mechanisms were planted and known. V65/V67 larger RNA/operator simulations (e.g. **5,200 cells, 104 donors, 42 operators, eight shared + four private factors**) tested recoverability and teacher/student mechanics; a supervised oracle-Z/EMA diagnostic was **not** a learned self-supervised JEPA biological teacher. Actual RBB learned-belief experiments had negative intrinsic information retention; a later RBB synthetic test-of-test did not reverse that biology.
2. **Measurement/operator and statistical falsification:** V75 qualified a **100K observation architecture**, not 500K scaling/biology. Frozen **V79 Q00–Q11 twelve worlds** exercised donor/source/rare/sparse/absent-operator and statistical estimates; **Q08 convergence hard failed**. The qualified **V80 eight-world /192 fit** package is a new estimator-independent *benchmark*, not a qualified estimator, biology or JEPA teacher. Later #533 separated terminal 207-case confirmatory scope from 85 forensic cases; #534 identified a **new cross-host V80 SHA identity failure**, not a reason to alter original frozen truth.
3. **Candidate-agnostic integrated RNA+ATAC/rare/perturbation rehearsal:** PR #315 designed a unified substrate that reused older planted biology, V79 operator controls and newer rare/multimodal/context/perturbation ingredients without planting C1–C6 as a shortcut. PR #328 actually qualified a narrower **480-cell × 48-address mechanics/interface fixture** with measured-zero versus structural absence, matched perturbation null, true controller/fit separation and reproducible SHA. It **did not** build the full 41K, Stage81A3 mechanistic/production target world. Synthetic controller truth is not the measured rich teacher and may not be copied into the student's prediction.
4. **Test-of-test, admission and actual integration:** PR #336 independently verified **9/9** known-truth relational controller statuses—**test quality**, not a learned representation. PR #369 ran a broader sealed cross-gate repair battery. PR #469 actually joined generated RNA to the existing Task5 fixture. PR #526 measured an eight-other-gene target on frozen synthetic observations; PR #531 replicated its arithmetic but invalidated the *source preference* that treated observed non-detection as structural missingness. PR #536 actually called the measured C3 producer, refused a fabricated student, and **correctly refused a 48-gene fixture as an input to the C2 3×512 measured-feature executor**. A green refusal is not a positive C2 result.
5. **Proposed but not executed new JEPA training:** PR #537 contains a GPU-only four-trajectory 500-step JEPA_ONLY proposal and CPU preflight; its scientific novelty relative to executed Stage81B must not be assumed. It is **not a run result**.

Across these systems the exact scientific distinction is: **(a)** planted truth, **(b)** a simulated measured target, **(c)** a synthetic learner fitting it, **(d)** known-truth test quality, and **(e)** human biological evidence are *different claims*. Success at an earlier level must not be promoted into later levels.

## Act VII — Source rights, implementation and review are the bridge, not the destination (October–November)

A biologically meaningful teacher/student experiment has to be able to prove **what each participant was actually allowed to see**. Later audits found false gene-feature equivalences, donor-row/specimen joins, BIN1 guide and PAR_Y comparator errors, QID/query misinterpretations and full-cell library denominator leakage. Importantly, `log1p(10000*count_g/full_cell_library)` contains information about a hidden queried gene even after that gene’s column is removed: the **denominator still changes**. The mathematical/software counterexample is real; its presence in any specific proposed model must be assessed by original execution evidence, not presumed.

The project consequently chose a **D1 student base-only depth** view over D2/full-cell depth; teacher-private physical totals and source labels are not unrestricted student inputs. It kept support semantics and gene-alias rights in an audited issuer, and distinguished *measured zero* from *not measured*. HVS and SEA-AD source feature accounting and public provenance are recovered; NPH52 query issuance remains restricted, even though public acquisition itself is not missing. SEA-AD `sample_id` cannot be treated as an independent tissue specimen when source audit shows it uniquely identifies nuclei. PR #59/69 original selected physical counts, donor UMI and labels were already read/repaired, so nothing here implies rerunning them.

**Software work is real:** The TD Task0–3 / post-TD/Task5/Task6–8 gates, source-to-CaseBundle contracts, known-truth tests, independent hostile Macha reviews, conservative alias restrictions and fail-closed student environment evolved through many PRs. Main PR #522–#524 has **scoped** independently reviewed D1/V2C/Docker student-fixture protections. That is neither a genuine original human-source issuer nor a trained student. PR #525/#538 contain additional author-reviewed/repaired controls requiring *fresh exact-head review if adopted*; PR #535 explicitly withdrew a redundant proposed producer/redactor fixture. PR #534's separate SHA problem and historic G6/G7 one-shot real-qualification receipts must remain in their exact scopes; historic G7 ended **NOT_ESTIMABLE** for structural sample disjointness, while the one spent PR #314 relational replay was executed and is not an open task.

**Operational split:** Macha owns GPU-laptop physical source/checkpoint execution and independent challenge; GPT owns scientific synthesis, code and independent assessment. Both can challenge each other. Completed GPU source and ETL work is *prior evidence*, not a reason for repeated whole-dataset rediscovery. Source evidence and real implementations live in their owner PRs. PR #322 is the **append-only scientific/engineering history**; PR #460 is the **up-to-date operational handoff**. A new agent must read/update both after meaningful work; avoid a separate history-only handoff branch.

## The real cross-cutting scientific thread

Five principles explain *all* major pivots:

1. **Disease relevance is the original reason for the work**, but pathology labels cannot be privileged shortcuts in the pathology-blind foundation learner. Pathology belongs in separately governed, held-donor validation and later biology.
2. **Cells are not vectors of exchangeable genes.** Molecular identity, gene-local and rare distributed evidence, regulatory priors and physical context matter. However, a richer feature or graph is only worth keeping if it earns measurable biological value against appropriate negative controls.
3. **Different assays are different observations of overlapping biology**, not interchangeable fully measured transcriptomes. Source/donor/technology and depth can dominate apparent biological signals. Shared state, private state, observation process and support all require honest estimands.
4. **Rich teacher information is not automatically student-learnable information.** Exact prediction of private biology from incomplete evidence can be unidentifiable. A real result requires both retained biology and an increment over source, depth, gene identity, global and wrong-cell/query shortcuts, not just optimization success.
5. **RNA → transferable state → independent regulatory support → perturbational validity → biological/therapeutic mechanism** is a ladder, not one score. Regulatory/ATAC/SCENIC+/CRISPR/spatial work was done and matters, but association and in-silico shifts remain distinct from confirmed causality.

## What is genuinely established as of November 4, 2026

**Established only in their original scope:** Internal pathology-related prediction and gene hypotheses; historic partial graph/rare tests and their failed stronger locks; 18-gene mechanism program; executed frozen GeneJEPA perturbation/graph-controls; real source and observation operator/identity mapping; historical gene-token and 224D lineage; original Stage81B anchor rejection and alignment-aware information retention; real broad/mesoscale RNA donor recurrence with failed finer universality; R5 developmental measured query result; substantial synthetic truth/measurement/known-test engineering; D1/V2C source-read-free fixture rights; first-party and external audited reviews/repairs. All original negatives remain material.

**Not established:** A selected C1–C6 human JEPA target; a production biological dimensionality/representation family; a genuinely learned biologically qualified EMA teacher; a lawful partial-evidence student that recovers independent biology; cross-source invariance; validated regulatory/causal/therapeutic effect; a full trusted real-source issuer merely because fixtures pass; Stage4 or real training permission.
