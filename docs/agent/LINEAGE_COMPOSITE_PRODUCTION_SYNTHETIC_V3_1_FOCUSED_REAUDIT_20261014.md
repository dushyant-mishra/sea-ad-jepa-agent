## Critical findings re-check

### B1 anti-gaming
PASS at design-text level. The V3.1 freeze is ordered before **any** downstream run from that generator version, removing the dev-result tuning loophole. Seed custody is commitment-based and domain-separated, and realization selection is constrained by predeclared budget plus producer attestation.

Remaining work is executable implementation/hostile testing only; this cannot be claimed qualified yet.

### B2 R1 estimand
PASS at design-text level. V3.1 names an expected-state estimand with a common coordinate set within each triplet and separates latent fidelity from the estimand. It also requires an asymmetric-depth same-biology counterfactual.

R1 same-code-path rehearsal remains correctly blocked because no frozen complete real executor/source-parameterized path exists.

### B3 evidence masking
PASS at design-text level. Rights masking is now a fourth state, the normalization denominator cannot use hidden totals, visible values are invariant across rights levels, and mislabeled thinning must be rejected.

G7 decision rehearsal remains external until the decision layer is independently frozen and qualified.

### B4 candidate-shaped biology
PASS at design-text level. Candidate/block names and structural mirrors are prohibited from generator mechanism vocabulary. Candidate mapping occurs controller-side after realization, with out-of-basis, wrong-basis and random-capacity controls required.
