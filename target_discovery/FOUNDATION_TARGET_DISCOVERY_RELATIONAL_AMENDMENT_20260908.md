# Foundation Target Discovery V1 — relational-protocol amendment after TD56

Status: `PROSPECTIVE_AMENDMENT__NO_TRAINING_AUTHORITY`
Date: 2026-09-08

## Purpose

The original `FOUNDATION_TARGET_DISCOVERY_PROSPECTIVE_CONTRACT_V1.md` was frozen before TD56 and assumes that successful target discovery must terminate in a fixed biological target tensor/module/subspace with a mathematical definition and dimension.

TD56 subsequently established a different surviving object class: label-free, donor-wise, cross-source **relational geometry** recovered from disjoint Molecular Ledger gene views, while repeated fixed-coordinate candidates failed.

This amendment does not rewrite the original contract. It prospectively adds a lawful relational-protocol route while preserving every population firewall, observation-state rule, donor-primary rule, null requirement, evidence/depth separation, transfer requirement, and fail-closed principle.

## Relational route

A Foundation candidate may now be a **qualified relational training protocol** rather than a named coordinate tensor, provided that:

1. the biological relational object exists above matched measurement/shortcut nulls;
2. recurrence is donor-primary and survives deterministic donor-block resampling;
3. the defining geometry survives disjoint molecular views;
4. the protocol does not require universal latent-axis identity or absolute cross-source distance scale;
5. partial-evidence student geometry is predictably related to rich-evidence teacher geometry beyond technical and fine-neighborhood wrong-cell nulls;
6. anti-collapse diagnostics and fine-structure preservation are separately qualification-bearing;
7. checkpoint acceptance is distinct from protocol qualification;
8. no protected population/pathology is opened;
9. production values are derived only from the complete lawful reader-fit population, not the 50k falsification archive or 3,292-cell mechanics corpus.
